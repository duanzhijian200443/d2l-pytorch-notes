I’d kept the old coat for years, though it no longer fit. Before donating it, I checked the pockets and found a faded cinema ticket. I couldn’t remember the film, but I remembered the friend beside me, the easy conversation afterward, how little we’d thought about time passing. For a moment, I wanted to keep the coat. Instead, I put the ticket in a drawer and folded the coat for someone else. It was the ticket I wanted to keep.
