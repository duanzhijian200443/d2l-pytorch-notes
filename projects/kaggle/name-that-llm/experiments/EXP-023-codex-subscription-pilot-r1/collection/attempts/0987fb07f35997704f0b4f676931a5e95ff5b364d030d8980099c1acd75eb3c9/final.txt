Build a flexible grocery list around **meal components**, not a rigid day-by-day schedule. First, check your pantry, fridge, freezer, and existing leftovers. Then choose three or four adaptable meals that share ingredients—for example, roasted vegetables can become tacos, grain bowls, pasta, or soup.

Organize the list into:

- **Proteins:** eggs, beans, chicken, tofu  
- **Bases:** rice, pasta, tortillas, bread  
- **Vegetables and fruit:** prioritize versatile, longer-lasting options  
- **Flavor extras:** sauces, herbs, cheese, spices  
- **Backup foods:** frozen vegetables, canned soup, or an easy freezer meal  

Buy enough for fewer dinners than the week contains, since leftovers and changed plans will fill some gaps. Midweek, reassess what remains and make a small top-up trip if necessary. This reduces waste while preserving choice and spontaneity.
