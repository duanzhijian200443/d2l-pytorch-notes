### Share Your Memories of the Old Swimming Pool

Do you remember learning to swim, meeting friends, or spending summer afternoons at the old swimming pool? We are inviting local residents to share their memories and help preserve this special part of our community’s history.

Taking part involves a relaxed interview lasting around twenty minutes. You can talk about any experiences, stories, or moments connected with the pool that are meaningful to you. Participation is entirely optional, and you only need to share what you feel comfortable discussing.

You can also decide whether you would like your name to appear alongside your contribution or whether you would prefer to remain anonymous.

Selected extracts from the interviews will be edited and displayed at the community center, giving residents an opportunity to discover the many ways the old swimming pool has been part of local life.

Whether your memories are funny, ordinary, vivid, or brief, we would be delighted to hear them. Your story can help bring our shared local history to life.
