Before committing, the cafe should gather evidence on likely demand: how many customers want extended hours, which days and times they prefer, and whether requests represent a broader customer group. It should estimate additional sales and compare them with staffing, utilities, security, cleaning, and inventory costs. Management should also review employee availability, overtime rules, local licensing or noise restrictions, nearby competitors’ hours, and safety considerations. A short trial period could provide real sales data and customer feedback before making the change permanent.
