Start with a simple notebook or phone note. For one week, spend 10 minutes each morning looking through the window and record what you notice: size, colors, beak shape, tail length, behavior, flight pattern, and where the bird sits. Take ordinary phone photos if possible, but don’t worry about perfect images.

Use a free bird-identification app or a local bird guide website to compare your observations. Begin with the five most frequent birds rather than trying to learn everything. Check one name at a time, and write a short description beside it.

Listen for repeated songs and use free sound recordings for comparison. Review your notes weekly, quiz yourself from photos, and gradually add new species. Avoid feeding birds unless local guidance permits it.
