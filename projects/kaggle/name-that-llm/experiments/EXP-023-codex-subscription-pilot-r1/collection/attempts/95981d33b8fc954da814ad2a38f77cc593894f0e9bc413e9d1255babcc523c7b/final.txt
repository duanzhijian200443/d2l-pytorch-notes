Try a “minimum viable reading” routine:

- **Set a tiny daily target:** Read for 10 minutes or five pages—whichever comes first. Make the goal so easy that busy days still count.
- **Anchor it to an existing habit:** Read after breakfast, during lunch, or before turning off the light. Keep the book visible and your phone elsewhere.
- **Use flexible formats:** Audiobooks during commuting or chores can supplement, not replace, quiet reading.
- **Choose one appealing book:** Avoid building an ambitious reading list. If a book feels like work after 30 pages, switch without guilt.
- **Protect one weekly session:** Reserve 20–30 minutes on the weekend for uninterrupted reading.
- **Track consistency, not volume:** Mark each day you read, even briefly. Missing a day requires no catch-up—just resume at the next opportunity.
