Captions and a written transcript both convert a recorded lecture’s speech into text, improving access, comprehension, and review. However, captions are synchronized with the video, so viewers can follow the speaker’s words, relevant sounds, and sometimes speaker changes as they occur. They primarily support people who are deaf or hard of hearing, as well as viewers in noisy or quiet environments. A transcript is a separate, searchable document that presents the lecture’s content in continuous text. It benefits learners who prefer reading, studying, quoting, or quickly locating specific information. Captions support real-time viewing; transcripts support flexible, detailed reference. Providing both gives learners equivalent access during playback and a convenient resource for later study.
