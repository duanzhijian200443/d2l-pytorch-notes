A short menu can be more helpful than a large one, but its value depends on what customers expect. For people who want a quick, easy decision, fewer options reduce confusion and save time. A short menu may also suggest that the restaurant focuses on dishes it prepares well, using fresh ingredients.

However, customers seeking variety may prefer a larger menu. Families, groups, and people with dietary restrictions often need more choices to find something suitable. Regular customers might also become bored if a small menu rarely changes.

Overall, a short menu works best when it offers a balanced selection and clear dietary information. A larger menu is useful when variety matters, provided the restaurant maintains quality across its dishes.
