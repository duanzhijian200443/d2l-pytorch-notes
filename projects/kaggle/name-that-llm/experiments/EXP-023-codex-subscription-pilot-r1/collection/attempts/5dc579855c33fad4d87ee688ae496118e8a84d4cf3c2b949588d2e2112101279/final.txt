Use one notebook at a time as an **indexed commonplace book**.

- Number every page (or buy a numbered notebook).
- Reserve the first 4–6 pages for an index.
- When starting a note, write the date and a clear project/topic label at the top.
- Add a short index entry: “Garden app ideas — pp. 24–27.”
- Use simple symbols: `•` note, `□` task, `★` important idea, `→` moved elsewhere.
- Keep a sticky tab or bookmark at the current page.
- For separate active projects, use colored page flags—not separate notebooks.
- Once a week, spend five minutes adding missed index entries and reviewing starred items.

When full, label the spine with a date range and notebook number. This makes every idea searchable without maintaining a complicated system.
