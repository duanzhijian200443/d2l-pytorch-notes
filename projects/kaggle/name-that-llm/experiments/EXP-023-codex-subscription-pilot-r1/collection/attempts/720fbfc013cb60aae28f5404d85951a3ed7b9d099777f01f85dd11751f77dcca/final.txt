Start by listing what the club must do with each registration: identify the member, contact them, confirm eligibility, and arrange payment if needed. For every proposed field, ask: “What specific action requires this information?” If there is no clear, current purpose, leave it out.

Essential fields might include a name, one contact method, membership type, and confirmation of relevant eligibility requirements. Collect an age range rather than a full birth date when that is sufficient. Avoid requesting addresses, occupations, or interests simply because they might be useful later.

Separate genuinely optional questions from required ones and explain why sensitive information is needed. Test the draft with prospective members, remove unnecessary questions, and review the form periodically as the club’s needs change.
