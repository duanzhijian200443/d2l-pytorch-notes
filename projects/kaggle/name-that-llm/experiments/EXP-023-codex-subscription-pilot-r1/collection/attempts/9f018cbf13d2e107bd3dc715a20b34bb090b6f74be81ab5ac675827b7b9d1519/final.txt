Keep the menu simple and mostly make-ahead: one generous main, a salad, good bread, and an easy dessert. Clear a little space, but don’t try to hide every sign of living. Use warm lighting, background music, and mismatched plates if needed. Set drinks where guests can help themselves. Serve family-style so nobody feels managed. Most importantly, sit down and join them—your relaxed presence will make the evening feel welcoming, not staged.
