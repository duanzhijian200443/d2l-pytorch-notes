Empty the cupboard and spread containers on a table, with lids beside them. Recycle or discard cracked, warped, or otherwise unusable pieces according to local rules. Group containers by shape, then size; do the same with lids. Match each container with a lid and check that it seals securely.

Set unmatched pieces aside in one small box. Give yourself a week to find their partners, then let the remaining orphans go. Keep only as many complete sets as your household realistically uses.

Nest containers by size and store lids upright in a divided bin. To prevent another buildup, choose one compatible container range, avoid keeping every takeaway tub, and follow a one-in, one-out rule. Check for mismatches monthly.
