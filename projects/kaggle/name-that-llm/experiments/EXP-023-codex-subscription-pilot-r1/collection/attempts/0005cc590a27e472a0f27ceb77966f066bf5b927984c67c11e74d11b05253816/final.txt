New members are warmly invited to attend one session free of charge and get a feel for the group. Tools are shared among participants, so please be considerate and return everything after use. Because storage is limited, we’re unable to keep unfinished projects between sessions—please plan to take your work with you. Our instructor is happy to answer questions, explain techniques, and offer guidance, but participants are responsible for completing their own projects. We look forward to welcoming you, helping you get started, and supporting your creative process in a friendly, collaborative environment.
