1. First, collect the key from the office before entering the room.  
2. When using the room, switch on only the lights that are needed. This saves energy and avoids unnecessary use of electricity.  
3. Use the space and its furniture carefully throughout your visit.  
4. If you notice that anything is damaged, broken, or not working properly, inform the caretaker as soon as possible.  
5. Before leaving, return all chairs to their usual position along the wall so the room is tidy and ready for the next users.  
6. Check that the windows are fully shut before you go, especially if the weather is poor or the room will be unattended.  
7. Finally, turn off the lights, lock the room if required, and return the key as instructed.
