A queue is like a line where everyone agrees to take turns. The first person who arrives gets helped first, then the next person, and so on. Waiting can feel slow when you really want something right away. But without a line, people might all rush toward the counter at once. That could make things noisy, confusing, and harder for the person helping everyone. A queue gives each person a clear place to stand and a simple way to know when their turn is coming. It is not about being the fastest or strongest. It is just an organized way to make sure people get helped one at a time.
