Before copying a competitor’s feature, the team should understand whether its own users actually have the same problem and whether the feature fits their goals. It should examine usage data, search queries, support tickets, churn reasons, and requests to identify unmet needs—not just popularity elsewhere. Interviews and usability tests can reveal users’ workflows, motivations, frustrations, and acceptable trade-offs. The team should also segment users: a feature valued by power users may confuse or burden beginners. Finally, it should assess willingness to adopt, pay, or switch behavior, and test a lightweight prototype with representative users. The goal is to validate the underlying user need and desired outcome, then determine whether copying—or designing a better, more context-appropriate solution—is justified.
