Reversible editing preserves the original image and records adjustments separately or in non-destructive layers. You can revisit, refine, disable, or remove edits without losing source detail, making it ideal for professional workflows and experimentation. Repeatedly overwriting the original permanently compounds changes: compression artifacts, lost pixels, and mistaken crops or retouching may become impossible to recover. Overwriting can be simpler and save storage, but it is risky. Keep originals intact and export flattened copies for sharing or delivery.
