Introduce a lightweight but firm change-request process:

1. **Log every request** in one shared tracker, regardless of size. Include the requested change, reason, requester, deadline, and affected deliverables.
2. **Assess impact before accepting it.** The project lead estimates added time, cost, dependencies, and risk.
3. **Require client approval of the trade-off.** Present options: extend the timeline, increase the budget, remove another deliverable, or defer the change to a later phase.
4. **Set a cutoff point.** After an agreed milestone, only critical corrections are accepted; preference changes enter a future work queue.
5. **Bundle minor requests.** Review them at a scheduled weekly checkpoint rather than interrupting work continuously.
6. **Document approval** in writing and update the project plan immediately.

This keeps the studio responsive while making cumulative impact visible and preventing “tiny” requests from silently derailing delivery.
