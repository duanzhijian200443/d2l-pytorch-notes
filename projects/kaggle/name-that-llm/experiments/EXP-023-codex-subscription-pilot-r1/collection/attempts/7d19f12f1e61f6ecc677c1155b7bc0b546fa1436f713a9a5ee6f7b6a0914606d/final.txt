A visually uniform central square can create a strong sense of harmony and shared identity. Coordinated signs, colours, and materials help historic buildings stand out, reduce visual clutter, and make public space feel carefully maintained. However, strict rules may suppress creativity and make independent businesses harder to distinguish.

Allowing every shop a distinctive sign brings personality, variety, and commercial energy. Signs can express owners’ backgrounds, communicate what businesses offer, and help visitors remember particular places. Yet unrestricted designs may compete for attention, overwhelm architecture, or create an untidy appearance.

A balanced policy could protect the square’s overall character through limits on size, lighting, and placement while leaving room for individual lettering, imagery, and colour. This would combine civic coherence with local creativity.
