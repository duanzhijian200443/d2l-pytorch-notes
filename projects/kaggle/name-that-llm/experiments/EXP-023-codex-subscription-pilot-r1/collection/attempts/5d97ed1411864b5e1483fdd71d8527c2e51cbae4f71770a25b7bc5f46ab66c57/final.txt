Welcome! If you’re new, you’re invited to try one session for free—a chance to meet the group, explore the space, and see whether it’s a good fit.

Tools are shared among participants, so please be ready to take turns and leave them ready for the next person. We’re unable to store unfinished projects, so plan to take your work home at the end of each session.

Our instructor is happy to answer questions and offer guidance as you learn. Your project remains your own, though: the instructor won’t finish work for you. Bring your curiosity, work at your own pace, and enjoy the satisfaction of making something yourself. We look forward to welcoming you!
