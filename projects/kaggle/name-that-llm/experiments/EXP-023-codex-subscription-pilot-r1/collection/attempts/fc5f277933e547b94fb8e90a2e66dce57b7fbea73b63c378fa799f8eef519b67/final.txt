Start with the birds you see most often, not a huge checklist. For two weeks, spend 5–10 minutes by the window each morning or late afternoon. Keep a simple note: date, size, main colors, beak shape, behavior, and call.

Use a free bird-ID app or local bird organization website to compare your observations. Learn just one or two species at a time, repeating their names aloud when you see them. Focus on clues visible without binoculars: “pigeon-sized,” “long tail,” “hops on ground,” or “perches on wires.”

Make a small “window birds” chart with photos and names. Review it daily. If possible, put out water only where it is safe and permitted, and keep windows marked to prevent bird strikes. Within a month, you’ll likely recognize your regular visitors confidently.
