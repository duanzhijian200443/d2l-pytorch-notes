Use **one main notebook plus a simple index**.

Reserve the first 4–6 pages for an index. Number every page. Whenever you add a note, write a short title at the top—such as “Garden redesign” or “App idea”—and record that title and page number in the index.

For projects, use a consistent symbol or short code, like **GARDEN**, **APP**, or **HOME**, in the top corner of each relevant page. Keep writing on the next available page rather than reserving sections; the index will connect scattered entries.

Carry one small “inbox” notebook if needed. Once a week, transfer worthwhile ideas into the main notebook and recycle or archive the inbox. When the main notebook is full, label its spine with a volume number and date range, then start the next one.
