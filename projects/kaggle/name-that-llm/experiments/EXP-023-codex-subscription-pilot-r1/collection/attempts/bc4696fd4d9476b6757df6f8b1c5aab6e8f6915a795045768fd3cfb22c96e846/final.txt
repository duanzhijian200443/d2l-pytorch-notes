The bookshelf instructions were in Finnish. Neither Mara nor the man in the raincoat spoke Finnish, but the hospital waiting room had grown unbearable.

“Hold this?” she asked.

He did.

They worked between vending machines, arranging dowels on yesterday’s newspaper. Once, they put a shelf in backward and laughed too loudly.

When a nurse called his name, he handed Mara the little hex key.

“You’ll finish?”

She nodded.

He returned twenty minutes later with two coffees. She had saved the last screw.
