Build your grocery list around flexible ingredients, not fixed meals. Start by checking what you already have, especially perishables and leftovers. Choose a few versatile staples—vegetables, grains, eggs, beans, chicken, or tofu—that can combine into several meals. Plan “meal components” rather than exact dishes: a cooked protein, roasted vegetables, rice, salad greens, and a sauce can become bowls, wraps, soups, or sides.

Add a small number of optional items for variety, and include backup foods for busy nights, such as frozen vegetables, pasta, canned beans, or soup. Estimate quantities for the week, but avoid buying too much fresh food. Leave one or two meals unplanned to use leftovers and accommodate changing schedules. Before shopping, review expiration dates and revise the list accordingly.
