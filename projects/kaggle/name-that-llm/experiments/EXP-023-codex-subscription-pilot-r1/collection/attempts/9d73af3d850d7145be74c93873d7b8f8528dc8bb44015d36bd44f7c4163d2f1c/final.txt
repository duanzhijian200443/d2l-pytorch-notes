Use a **three-pass selection method**:

1. **Group similar photos** by event, date, location, or person. Phone photo apps can often do this automatically.
2. **Remove obvious rejects**: blurry shots, accidental screenshots, duplicates, closed eyes, and poor expressions.
3. **Choose one “winner” per moment**. Compare similar images and keep the photo with the strongest expression, composition, lighting, or emotional value.

Then create a temporary **“Print Candidates” album**. Review it again after a few days and ask: Does this photo tell a story, capture someone important, or make me feel something? Favor variety—people, places, everyday life, and milestones—rather than many versions of one scene.

Finally, set a fixed limit, such as 50 prints per year. A clear quota makes the last decisions much easier.
