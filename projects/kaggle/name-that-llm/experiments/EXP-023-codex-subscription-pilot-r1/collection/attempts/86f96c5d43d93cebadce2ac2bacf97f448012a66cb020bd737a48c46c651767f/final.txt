The sign states a one-way relationship: **if someone is a club member, then they own a bicycle**. It does not state the reverse—that anyone who owns a bicycle must be a club member. Many people outside the club may also own bicycles. For example, Alice may belong to the club and own a bicycle, while Ben owns a bicycle but has never joined the club. Thus, club membership implies bicycle ownership, but bicycle ownership alone does not imply club membership.
