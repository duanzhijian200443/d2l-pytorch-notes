The first room starts with **12 chairs**.

First, **4 chairs are moved to the second room**. Subtract those chairs from the original total:

**12 − 4 = 8**

So, after the first move, **8 chairs remain in the first room**, while 4 chairs are in the second room.

Next, **3 of those chairs are brought back** from the second room to the first room. Add those 3 chairs to the 8 still in the first room:

**8 + 3 = 11**

Therefore, after both steps are completed, **11 chairs remain in the first room**. The intermediate total is important: the room goes from 12 chairs to 8 chairs when 4 are removed, and then from 8 chairs to 11 chairs when 3 are returned.
