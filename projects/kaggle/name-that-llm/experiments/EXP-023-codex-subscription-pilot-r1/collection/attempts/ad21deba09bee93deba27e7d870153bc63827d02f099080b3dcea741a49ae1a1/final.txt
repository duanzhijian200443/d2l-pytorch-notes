Before using the room, go to the office and collect the key. When you enter, switch on only the lights you actually need to avoid wasting electricity. Use the room and its furniture carefully. If you notice that anything is damaged, or if damage occurs while you are there, inform the caretaker as soon as possible. When you have finished, return all chairs to their proper places along the wall, leaving the room neat and orderly. Before leaving, check every window and make sure it is fully shut. Switch off all the lights you used, then do a final check to ensure the room is secure and tidy. Finally, leave the room and return the key to the office as instructed.
