A garden built around a strict visual design offers order, symmetry, and predictable color or height combinations. It can look polished and make maintenance easier, but it may require frequent pruning, spacing adjustments, and replacement of plants that grow unpredictably. Allowing plants to spread naturally creates a more relaxed, wildlife-friendly ecosystem with greater seasonal variation and less intervention. However, it can appear untidy, compete for resources, and make pathways or plant relationships harder to control.
