Mara painted a wooden sign for her tiny roadside bakery. She meant to write, “Fresh Bread Every Morning,” but after working late, she accidentally painted, “Fresh Bread Every Mourning.”

The next day, a customer noticed and smiled. “A comforting loaf for sad days?” he asked.

Mara laughed and decided not to fix it. Soon, neighbors began stopping by whenever they needed cheering up. They brought flowers, shared stories, and left with warm bread tucked under their arms.

Someone posted a picture of the sign online, and before long, visitors came from nearby towns to see it. Mara’s bakery became locally famous—not for perfect spelling, but for kindness.

Years later, the sign still hung above the door, its little mistake reminding everyone that warmth can rise from unexpected places.
