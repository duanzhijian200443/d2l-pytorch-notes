Try a five-minute “closing shift”: write down tomorrow’s first task, close your laptop, and tuck work items into a basket or drawer. Wipe the table, then change one detail—add a placemat, flowers, or a lamp with warmer light. Take a short walk or stretch before sitting down again. Changing clothes or putting on a favorite playlist can also signal the switch. Keep it simple and repeatable: the goal is a clear transition, not another chore.
