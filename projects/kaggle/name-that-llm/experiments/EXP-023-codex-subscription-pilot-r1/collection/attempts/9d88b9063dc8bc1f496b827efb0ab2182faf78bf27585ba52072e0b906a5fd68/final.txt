Count each handshake as a pair of people, rather than counting it separately for each person. The first person shakes hands with four others. The second has three new handshakes, since the handshake with the first person is already counted. The third adds two, the fourth adds one, and the fifth adds none. Thus, the total is 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = **10 handshakes**. This method counts every pair exactly once, avoiding double-counting.
