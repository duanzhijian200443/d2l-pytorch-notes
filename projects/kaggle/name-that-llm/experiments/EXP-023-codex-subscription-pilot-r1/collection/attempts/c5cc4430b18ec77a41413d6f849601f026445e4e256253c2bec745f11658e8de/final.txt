A visually uniform central square creates harmony, order, and a strong shared identity. Matching signs, colours, and materials can make historic architecture easier to appreciate, reduce visual clutter, and give visitors a calm, memorable impression. It may also help the town present a clear brand.

Distinctive shop signs, however, add character and energy. They allow local owners to express their businesses, make streets easier to navigate, and encourage curiosity: a handmade bakery sign or unusual bookshop display can become part of the town’s charm. Too much variety can feel chaotic, while excessive uniformity can seem impersonal. The best solution is often a balance: common rules for size, lighting, and quality, alongside freedom for individual design.
