A 10% discount on an item priced at 100 units reduces the price by 10 units, leaving 90 units. Then adding 10% means adding 10% of the reduced price, not the original price: 10% of 90 is 9 units. The final price is therefore 90 + 9 = 99 units. It is not the original 100 because the discount and increase are calculated from different bases. Percentages are not automatically reversible when applied successively.
