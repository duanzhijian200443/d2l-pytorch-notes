A queue is a line of people waiting for their turn. Everyone agrees that the person at the front goes first, and new people join at the back. Waiting can feel slow, especially when you are excited or in a hurry. But without a queue, everyone might rush toward the counter at the same time. People could bump into one another, argue, or try to squeeze ahead. Then the strongest or fastest person might get served first, while others are pushed aside. A queue makes the order clear, so nobody has to compete to reach the counter. Each person can see where they are in the line and know that their turn is coming, even if they have to wait a little while.
