Run a one-week demand test using a simple tally sheet. Record how many of each item are made, sold, and left over, plus when items sell out. Ask customers who miss the sold-out item what they wanted and whether they bought something else. Offer a paper or social media preorder form to capture unmet demand. Then slightly increase the popular item and reduce one leftover-heavy item, comparing sales and waste on similar weekdays before making broader menu changes.
