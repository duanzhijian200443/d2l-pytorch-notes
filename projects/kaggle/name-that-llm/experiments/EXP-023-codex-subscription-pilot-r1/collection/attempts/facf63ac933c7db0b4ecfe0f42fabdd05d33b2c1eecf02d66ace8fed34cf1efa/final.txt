An item priced at 100 units receives a 10% discount, subtracting 10 units and leaving 90 units. Adding 10% to that reduced price adds only 9 units, because the increase is calculated from 90, not 100. The final price is therefore 99 units, which is 1 unit below the original price. The percentages do not cancel because they apply to different amounts: the discount uses the original price, while the increase uses the smaller, discounted price.
