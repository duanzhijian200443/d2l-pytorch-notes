Mara was delivering noodles when she turned onto Willow Street instead of Willard. Halfway down the quiet block, she heard a faint metallic clatter.

An elderly man stood beside a storm drain, staring helplessly through the grate. “My keys,” he sighed. “Dropped them while watering the flowers.”

Mara parked her bicycle and examined the drain. From her delivery bag, she pulled a spare bungee cord with a tiny metal hook. After three careful tries, she lifted the key ring from the shallow water.

The man beamed. “You’ve saved our evening. Those keys open the community garden shed.”

Soon, neighbors were collecting chairs for their weekly gathering.

Mara delivered the noodles five minutes late—but with a free slice of lemon cake tucked into her bag for the ride home.
