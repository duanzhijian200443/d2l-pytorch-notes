A queue is a line of people waiting for their turns, like when you’re buying an ice cream. Everyone follows the same plan: the person at the front goes next, and the people behind wait until they reach the front.

When you’re standing in the line, it can feel very slow. You might think, “Why can’t I get my ice cream right now?” But imagine if there were no line. Everyone might rush toward the counter at once, trying to squeeze past each other. Getting served could depend on who could push through the crowd.

A queue makes it simpler: instead of competing to reach the counter, each person has a place and knows when their turn is coming.
