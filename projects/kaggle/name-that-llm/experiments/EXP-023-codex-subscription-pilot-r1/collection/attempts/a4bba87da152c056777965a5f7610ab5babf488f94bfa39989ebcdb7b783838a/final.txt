Start by defining the form’s immediate purpose: enrolling a person, confirming eligibility, communicating about club activities, and collecting payment or consent if required. Map each purpose to the minimum information needed—for example, name, reliable contact details, membership category, and any legally necessary consent. For every proposed field, ask: “What specific decision or action requires this information now?” If there is no clear answer, remove it.

Consult the people who process registrations, review applicable privacy requirements, and test a draft with prospective members. Mark only truly necessary fields as required; make helpful but nonessential questions optional. Avoid collecting sensitive details unless essential, and explain why they are needed. Finally, set retention and deletion rules. It is safer and easier to request additional information later when a genuine need arises than to store speculative data indefinitely.
