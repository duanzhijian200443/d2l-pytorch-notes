In the empty apartment, Mara found a man wrestling with a flat-pack bookshelf.

“Wrong unit?” she asked.

“Helping a friend,” he said. “You?”

“Waiting for the plumber next door.”

Neither knew where the shelves belonged, but together they decoded the diagrams, lost a screw, found it beneath his coat, and built the thing slightly crooked. When they finished, evening light filled its bare compartments.

“Looks lonely,” Mara said.

He placed the instruction booklet on one shelf.

“There,” he smiled. “Its first book.”
