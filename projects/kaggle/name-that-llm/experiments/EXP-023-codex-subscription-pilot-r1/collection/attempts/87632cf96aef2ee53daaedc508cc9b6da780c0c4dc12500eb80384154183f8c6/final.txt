Providing identical materials promotes fairness because everyone begins with the same resources and faces similar limits. It can also simplify planning, budgeting, and instruction for organizers. However, uniform supplies may restrict participants whose ideas require different tools or media.

Allowing people to bring their own materials encourages creativity and personal expression. Participants can use familiar items, experiment with unusual resources, and tailor projects to their interests. Yet this approach may feel unfair if some people can afford expensive equipment while others cannot. It may also create logistical problems, since organizers cannot predict what support or safety guidance will be needed.

A balanced option is to provide a basic shared kit while allowing optional personal additions.
