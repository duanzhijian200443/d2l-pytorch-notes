The curtains opened with the promise of morning and revealed a brick wall three feet away.

At first, the guest laughed. Then he checked the booking photo again: *city view*. Technically, a thin blue ribbon of sky appeared above the wall if he stood on tiptoe beside the kettle.

Over coffee, though, the wall began to offer details. One brick was chipped like a missing tooth. A spider had fastened its web between two drainpipes. On the fourth floor opposite, someone opened a window and placed a bowl of milk on the sill. Minutes later, a ginger cat arrived, balancing along the ledge with practiced calm.

By checkout, the guest had seen no skyline. Still, he found himself waiting for the cat.
