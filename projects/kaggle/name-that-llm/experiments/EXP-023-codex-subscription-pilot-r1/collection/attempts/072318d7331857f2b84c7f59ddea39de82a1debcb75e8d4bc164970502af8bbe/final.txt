Try choosing recipes through an **ingredient-first system** rather than shopping for each dish from scratch. Start by listing what you already have—especially perishables and opened specialty items—then select recipes that use several of them. Plan a week around overlapping ingredients: for example, herbs, yogurt, chickpeas, and lemons can work across curries, salads, dips, and grain bowls.

When buying something unfamiliar, choose recipes that use it in different ways, or check whether it freezes well. Favor flexible “template” meals—soups, stir-fries, tacos, pasta, and frittatas—where substitutions are easy. Keep a small, versatile pantry and borrow, share, or buy tiny quantities of rarely used spices. Finally, schedule a regular “use-it-up” meal to turn leftovers into something creative. This keeps experimentation fun while reducing waste, cost, and cupboard clutter.
