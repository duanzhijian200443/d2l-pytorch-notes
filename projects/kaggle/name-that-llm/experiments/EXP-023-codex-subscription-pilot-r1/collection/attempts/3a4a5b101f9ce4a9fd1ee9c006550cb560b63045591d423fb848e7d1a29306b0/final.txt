Pack around a simple layering system so you can adjust quickly without overfilling the backpack. Wear your bulkiest items while traveling: comfortable walking shoes, long pants, a T-shirt, and a lightweight sweater or fleece.

Inside the bag, pack two additional T-shirts, one extra pair of underwear and socks for each day, lightweight sleepwear, and one spare pair of pants or shorts depending on the forecast. Choose quick-drying fabrics so you can wash something in a sink if needed.

Bring a compact rain jacket that can also serve as a wind layer. If temperatures may drop, add a thin insulated jacket or warm base layer rather than a heavy coat. A small cap or beanie takes little room and can be surprisingly useful.

Use packing cubes or zip bags to compress clothing and keep categories separate. Roll clothes instead of folding them. Put toiletries in travel-size containers in a clear pouch, and limit yourself to essentials: toothbrush, toothpaste, deodorant, medication, sunscreen, and any necessary skincare.

Keep valuables and frequently used items accessible: phone charger, wallet, ID, tickets, reusable water bottle, snacks, and a small power bank. Leave “just in case” items behind unless they serve multiple purposes.
