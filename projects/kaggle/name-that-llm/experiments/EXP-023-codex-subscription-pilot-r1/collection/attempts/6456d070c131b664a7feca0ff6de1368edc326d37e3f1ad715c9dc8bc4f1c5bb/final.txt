A phone first uses its camera to capture the QR code, then image-processing software detects the square pattern, corrects perspective, and decodes the encoded data. This is the **reading** step: the phone has learned what the code contains, such as text, a URL, contact details, or Wi-Fi settings. **Opening the destination** is separate. If the decoded data is a URL, the scanning app may show a preview and ask for permission; when selected, it launches a browser or relevant app. The phone then may access the internet, resolve the domain, and load the destination. Thus, scanning can work offline, while reaching a linked website generally requires a network connection and user action.
