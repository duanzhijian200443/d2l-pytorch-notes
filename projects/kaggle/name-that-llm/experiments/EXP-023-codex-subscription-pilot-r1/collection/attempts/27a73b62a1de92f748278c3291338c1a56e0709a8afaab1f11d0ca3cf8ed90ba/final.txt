Welcome! New members are invited to attend one session at no cost—a great chance to explore the space and see how we work. Our tools are shared so that everyone has access, and we appreciate your help in keeping them available and organized for the whole community.

Please plan to take your projects home after each session, as we’re unable to store unfinished work on site. Our instructor is happy to answer questions, offer guidance, and help you build confidence with new skills. To ensure everyone receives support and has time to learn, participants remain responsible for completing their own projects. We’re excited to have you join us!
