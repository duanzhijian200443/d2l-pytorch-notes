The shortest route on a map may not be the best usable route because maps often measure distance or travel time without reflecting current access conditions. If the direct path between the first two stops passes through a closed gate, the driver must detour, even if that route is geographically shortest. A slightly longer alternative using open roads may be faster, safer, and legally accessible. Route planning should therefore consider closures, restrictions, traffic, road quality, and vehicle access—not distance alone.
