Audience arrangement shapes intimacy, focus, and power. A traditional proscenium layout separates performers from spectators, encouraging observation and reinforcing the “fourth wall.” Theatre-in-the-round or thrust staging brings actors closer, making spectators feel involved and allowing performers to respond to visible reactions. Immersive or promenade layouts can remove clear boundaries altogether, turning audience members into participants. Seating height, distance, lighting, and whether spectators face one another also influence shared attention and collective emotion.
