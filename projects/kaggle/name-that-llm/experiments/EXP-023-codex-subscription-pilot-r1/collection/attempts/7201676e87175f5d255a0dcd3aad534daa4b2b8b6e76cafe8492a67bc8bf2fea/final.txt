Prioritize comfort and easy upkeep over filling every corner.

1. **Start with one comfortable seat.** Choose a compact chair or bench with a weather-resistant frame. Add a small folding table for coffee or meals, leaving a clear route to the door.

2. **Choose materials that tolerate your weather.** Powder-coated aluminum and outdoor-rated recycled plastic generally need little care. Avoid untreated wood if you dislike regular refinishing. Use washable cushions and keep them in a dry spot when not needed.

3. **Keep planting simple.** Two or three larger pots usually dry out more slowly than many tiny ones. Choose hardy plants suited to your balcony’s sunlight, wind, and climate. Self-watering containers can reduce watering, but still need occasional checks.

4. **Make cleaning effortless.** Keep drainage clear, minimize floor-level clutter, and skip rugs if they tend to stay damp.

5. **Add one finishing touch.** A rechargeable outdoor lantern creates atmosphere without much upkeep. Secure anything wind could lift, and check building rules before attaching screens or planters.
