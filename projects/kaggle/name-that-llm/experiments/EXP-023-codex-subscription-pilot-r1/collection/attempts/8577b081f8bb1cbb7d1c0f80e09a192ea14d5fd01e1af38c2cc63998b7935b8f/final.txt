Before committing, the cafe should gather evidence about likely demand: how many customers requested longer hours, their preferred days and times, and expected visit frequency. It should estimate additional sales and compare them with labor, utilities, security, supply, and cleaning costs. Staff availability, willingness to work late, and wage implications should be assessed. The cafe should also review local regulations, competitor hours, safety concerns, and operational capacity. A limited trial, followed by customer and financial evaluation, could reduce risk before making the change permanent.
