A workshop that provides identical materials is often fairer because everyone starts with the same resources, regardless of their financial situation or access to supplies. It also makes instructions clearer and simplifies planning. However, uniform materials may limit creativity, since participants cannot choose tools or objects that reflect their personalities and experience. Allowing people to bring whatever they have encourages experimentation and can lead to more original results. It is also convenient for organizers because they do not need to purchase and prepare everything, although participants may forget items or feel disadvantaged if they lack suitable materials. A balanced approach may work best: provide essential supplies for everyone, while allowing participants to bring optional personal materials. This combines fairness and convenience with freedom and creativity.
