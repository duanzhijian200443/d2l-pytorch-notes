Before extending hours, gather evidence of likely demand: how many customers would use the café, which days and times they prefer, and expected spending per visit. Review local foot traffic, nearby businesses, competitors’ hours, and seasonal patterns. Calculate extra staffing, utilities, security, stock, cleaning, and waste costs. Check staff availability, licensing or lease restrictions, and safety considerations. Consider a short trial period and measure sales, customer feedback, and profitability before making the change permanent.
