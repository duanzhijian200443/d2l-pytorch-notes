Canvas awnings shade the narrow aisles of the small market, their edges lifting now and then in the breeze. Near the entrance, the smell of coffee hangs in the air, greeting shoppers before they reach the stalls. A woman stands behind a spread of pears, arranging them by size. She moves one from the middle to the end of a row, then pauses to compare two in her hands. Nearby, someone crouches beside a stall, tightening a loose wheel while people step around the tools. Above the gate, the clock is ten minutes fast. From beneath it, there is a clear view down the aisle: canvas overhead, pears on the counter, and a pair of feet beside the troublesome wheel.
