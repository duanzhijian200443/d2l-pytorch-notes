## Share Your Memories of Our Old Swimming Pool

We are collecting local memories and stories about the old swimming pool and would love to hear from people who used, worked at, or remember it.

We are inviting residents to take part in a short recorded interview, lasting around 20 minutes. You might share favourite memories, everyday moments, special events, or what the pool meant to you and the community.

Taking part is completely optional, and you can decide how much you would like to share. Contributors may choose whether to be named or remain anonymous.

Selected extracts from the interviews will be edited and displayed at the community centre as part of a public exhibition celebrating the pool’s place in local life.

If you would like to contribute, please speak to a member of the project team at the community centre.
