Giving everyone identical materials makes a workshop fairer because participants have the same resources, regardless of income or access to supplies. It also helps organizers plan activities and explain techniques consistently. However, buying and distributing materials can be costly, and uniform supplies may limit experimentation.

Letting people bring whatever they have encourages variety, personal expression, and creative reuse. It can reduce organizers’ expenses and allow participants to work with familiar tools. Yet some people may arrive with better equipment, while others may forget supplies or struggle to obtain them. Different materials can also make instructions harder to follow.

A balanced approach is to provide a basic kit for everyone while welcoming optional extras, combining equal access with creative freedom.
