Mara painted a sign for her little bakery: “Fresh Pies and Pastrys.” She noticed the missing “i” only after hanging it above the door.

“It has character,” her grandfather said.

Soon, neighbors began calling it the Pastrys Bakery. Children posed beneath the sign, and visitors stopped in just to see the famous mistake. Mara considered fixing it, but every week someone brought a story about how the cheerful typo had made them smile.

So she left it there, carefully touching up the faded letters each spring. The pies were delicious, the pastries were warm, and the imperfect sign reminded everyone that small errors can sometimes become beloved landmarks.
