Every morning, Ruth announced the same twelve departures. On Tuesday, she found a thirteenth in the timetable: 10:17 to Bell Street.

“Never been a train to Bell Street,” said the ticket clerk. “You can walk there.”

The driver shook his head. So did the cleaner, who had worked there forty years.

At 10:17, Ruth made no announcement. She went outside and looked along the platform.

Behind a loose timetable frame, she found a child’s drawing: a train, a house, and the words DAD HOME 10:17.
