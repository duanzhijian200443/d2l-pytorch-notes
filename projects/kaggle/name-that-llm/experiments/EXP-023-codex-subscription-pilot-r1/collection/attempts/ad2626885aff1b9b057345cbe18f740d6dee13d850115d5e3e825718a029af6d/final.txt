Try choosing recipes around what you already own, rather than buying a new set of ingredients for every dish. Before planning meals, check your cupboards, fridge, and freezer, and pick two or three ingredients to use up.

Build a week of varied meals with overlapping ingredients: chickpeas could become a curry, a salad, and crispy taco filling. Keep experimenting, but choose recipes that introduce only one unfamiliar ingredient at a time—and find a second use for it before buying.

Buy small quantities of specialty spices from bulk shops when available, and use sensible substitutions instead of purchasing another sauce or seasoning. Keep a simple “use next” list somewhere visible. You’ll preserve the fun of discovery while reducing waste, clutter, and spending.
