The percentage increase and decrease differ because they use different starting amounts. Attendance rose from 20 to 30, a gain of 10 people. Relative to the original 20, that is 10 ÷ 20 × 100 = 50%. When attendance fell from 30 to 20, the loss was also 10 people, but the starting amount was now 30. Thus, the decrease was 10 ÷ 30 × 100 ≈ 33.3%. The same change represents a smaller percentage of a larger starting amount.
