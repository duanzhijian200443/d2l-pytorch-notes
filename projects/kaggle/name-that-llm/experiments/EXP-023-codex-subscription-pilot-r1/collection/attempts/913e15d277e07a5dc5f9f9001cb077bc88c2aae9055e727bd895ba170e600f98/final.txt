At closing time, the bell above the door gave one soft chime. Mr. Vale stepped in, as he had every Thursday for thirty-seven years, though the shelves were bare now and the sign said **LAST DAY**. He bought nothing. Instead, he walked slowly through the familiar aisles, touching the counter, the dusty blue tin, the place where postcards had stood. The owner locked up behind him. Outside, they waved goodbye beneath the darkened window, both pretending they might return next week.
