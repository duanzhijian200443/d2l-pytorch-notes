Audience arrangement shapes how closely spectators connect with performers. In a proscenium theatre, the audience faces the stage from one direction, creating a clear separation and encouraging observation. Thrust or arena staging places spectators around or near the action, increasing intimacy, immediacy, and interaction. It also lets audience members see one another’s reactions, creating a shared experience. Flexible or promenade arrangements can further blur boundaries, making spectators feel like participants rather than passive observers.
