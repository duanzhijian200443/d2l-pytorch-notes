A queue is a way for people to take turns. Everyone lines up, and the person at the front goes first. Then the next person gets a turn, followed by the next one. Waiting can feel slow, especially when you are eager to reach the counter. But without a queue, everyone might rush forward at once. People could bump into each other, argue about who was there first, or try to push closer. The queue gives everyone a clear place and a clear order. You do not have to compete or be the strongest person in the room. You simply wait until the people ahead of you have finished, and then it is your turn.
