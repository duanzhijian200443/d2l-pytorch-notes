An accessible reading order is a logical sequence in which a document’s content is presented to assistive technologies, such as screen readers. It should preserve meaning by keeping headings, paragraphs, lists, captions, and related information in the correct order. Visual placement alone does not guarantee this because the underlying document structure may differ from what appears on screen. For example, text arranged in columns might be read across both columns rather than down each one unless its structure and reading order are properly defined.
