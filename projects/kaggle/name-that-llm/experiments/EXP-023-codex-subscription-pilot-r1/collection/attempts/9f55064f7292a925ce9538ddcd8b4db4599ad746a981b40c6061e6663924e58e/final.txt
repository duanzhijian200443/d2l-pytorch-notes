Use the existing floor space strategically: designate one slim shoe zone along the wall farthest from the door, keeping a clear path at least shoulder-width wide. Have each roommate place only their everyday pair there; store seasonal or extra shoes inside bedrooms. Arrange shoes toe-to-wall in a single line, or nest pairs heel-to-toe to save space. Add a simple shared rule: shoes come off immediately and get aligned, with the entryway reset nightly. Rotate pairs as needed rather than letting them accumulate.
