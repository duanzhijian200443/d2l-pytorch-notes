To scale the recipe from four people to six, multiply every ingredient by the same factor. The scaling factor is \(6 \div 4 = 1.5\). Therefore, multiply the three cups of broth by 1.5:

\[
3 \times 1.5 = 4.5
\]

You will need **4.5 cups of broth**, or **4½ cups**. This preserves the original proportion of broth to servings: the recipe uses \(3 \div 4 = 0.75\) cup per person, and \(0.75 \times 6 = 4.5\) cups. In practical cooking terms, you can measure four cups plus one-half cup. The same 1.5 multiplier should be applied to all the other ingredients so the flavor and consistency remain balanced for six people.
