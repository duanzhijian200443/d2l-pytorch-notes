To avoid counting any handshake twice, count by assigning each person a turn. The first person shakes hands with the other four, the second person then shakes only with the remaining three, the third with the remaining two, and the fourth with the last one. The fifth has no new handshakes left. Thus, the total is \(4+3+2+1=10\). Equivalently, choose 2 people from 5: \(\binom{5}{2}=10\).
