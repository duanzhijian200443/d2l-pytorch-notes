Keep it simple and slightly imperfect. Serve one generous, mostly make-ahead dish—pasta, stew, or a big roasted tray—plus good bread and a salad. Let guests help themselves to drinks and perch wherever there’s space: chairs, cushions, even the edge of the bed if appropriate. Dim the lights, make a playlist, clear only the essentials, and don’t apologize for the apartment. Warmth, conversation, and an unhurried host matter more than a polished setup.
