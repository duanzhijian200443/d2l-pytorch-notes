## Repair Afternoon

Have a small household item that’s stopped working? Bring it along to the community hall on Sunday, between 2 and 5 pm, for a repair afternoon.

Volunteers will take a look and explain what they try, giving you a chance to learn more about your item and the repair process. There’s no guarantee that it can be fixed, but you’ll be able to hear what’s been attempted and ask questions along the way.

Tea will be available, and there’s no need to book in advance—just drop in during the afternoon with your small broken household item.

**When:** Sunday, 2–5 pm  
**Where:** Community hall  
**Bring:** A small broken household item  
**Booking:** No advance booking needed
