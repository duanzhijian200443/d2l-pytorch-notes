I had kept the old coat for years, though it no longer fit. While emptying its pockets, I found a faded cinema ticket and was surprised that I could not remember the film at all. What returned instead was the friend beside me—their laughter, our conversation afterward, the feeling of being young and unhurried. I slipped the ticket into a drawer, unwilling to lose that small doorway to the past, and donated the coat. Some memories need less space than others.
