Use a **three-pass shortlist** rather than judging every photo individually.

1. **Choose a purpose.** Are the prints for an album, a wall, or gifts? Set a limit—say, 30 photos—to make decisions easier.
2. **Browse by event or month.** Quickly favorite pictures that spark a memory or feeling. Ignore near-duplicates for now, and work in short sessions.
3. **Pick one from each cluster.** Compare similar shots side by side. Favor meaningful expressions, clear subjects, and pleasing light over technical perfection.

Review the finalists together: include a mix of people, places, everyday moments, and milestones rather than only posed highlights. Check resolution and cropping before ordering. If you’re unsure about print quality, order a small test batch first.
