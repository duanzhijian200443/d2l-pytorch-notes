Pack by planning a small, mix-and-match wardrobe rather than one outfit per day. Wear your bulkiest items—comfortable shoes, long pants, and a light jacket—during travel. In the backpack, bring two lightweight shirts, one extra pair of underwear and socks for each day, and one pair of versatile trousers or shorts. Choose quick-drying, wrinkle-resistant fabrics so you can wash something in a sink if necessary.

For changing weather, pack a thin sweater or fleece, a compact waterproof shell, and a small umbrella if rain is likely. Layering is more efficient than carrying one heavy coat. Include sleepwear that can double as loungewear, plus minimal toiletries in travel-size containers. Solid soap, toothpaste tablets, and a small microfiber towel can save space.

Use packing cubes or rolled clothing to organize the bag and compress soft items. Put heavier objects close to your back, and keep frequently needed things—passport, wallet, phone charger, snacks, and medications—in accessible pockets. A reusable water bottle and a foldable tote are useful without taking much room.

Before leaving, check the forecast, remove “just in case” items, and wear anything that serves multiple purposes. Aim to leave some space for souvenirs and ensure essentials remain reachable without unpacking everything.
