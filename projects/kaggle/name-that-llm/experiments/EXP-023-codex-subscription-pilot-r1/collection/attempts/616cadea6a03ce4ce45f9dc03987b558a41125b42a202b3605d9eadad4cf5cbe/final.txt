Mara found the note while shelving returns on a wet Tuesday.

It had slipped behind the dust jacket of *The Secret Garden*, folded twice, the pencil faded but legible.

*To whoever reads this: I’m nine. I come here when Mum works late. What do you like best about being alone?*

Below it, in different handwriting:

*The quiet before someone comes looking for you.*

Then another response, dated three years later. Then another. Small reports from strangers: exams, a lost dog, a first apartment. The book had kept them all.

Mara added a final line.

*I used to sit by the radiator near the atlases. I’m still learning to like being alone.*

A week later, an envelope waited at the desk.

*I wrote the first reply,* it said. *I remember the radiator. Tuesday afternoons? I’ll be there at four.*

At four, an older woman sat beside the atlases with two paper cups of tea.

Neither spoke at once. Then the woman moved one cup across the table.
