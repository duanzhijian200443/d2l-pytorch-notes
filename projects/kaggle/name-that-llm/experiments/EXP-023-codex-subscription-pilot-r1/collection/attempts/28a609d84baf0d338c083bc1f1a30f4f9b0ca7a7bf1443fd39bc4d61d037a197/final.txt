The retired mapmaker unfolded a paper map across the café table.

“Is that a very large board game?” Maya asked.

He laughed. “It’s a map of the country.”

“But where’s the blue dot showing me?”

“Nowhere. You must find yourself.”

Maya traced the twisting roads. “How did people know where to go?”

“We watched landmarks, read signs, and trusted our sense of direction.”

She frowned. “What if you got lost?”

“Then the map became a conversation starter.”

He showed her contour lines, explaining how hills rose from the page like invisible waves. Maya turned the map upside down.

“North is the other way,” he said.

“Maybe,” she replied. “But this way looks more interesting.”

The mapmaker smiled. “That,” he said, “is how adventures begin.”
