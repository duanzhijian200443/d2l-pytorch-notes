There are 15 books on the shelf: ten novels and five biographies. The chance of selecting a biography is therefore 5 out of 15, or \(5/15 = 1/3\), approximately 33.3%. This means that, over many random selections with the book returned each time, about one-third of the selections would be biographies. One assumption is that every book has an equal chance of being chosen; for example, the person does not favor books because of their size, position, or cover.
