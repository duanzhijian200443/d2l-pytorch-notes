Every morning, Mara announced the commuter trains at Brookfield Station. One rainy Tuesday, she found a timetable tucked behind the microphone: **12:17—Northbound, Platform 3**. No one remembered the train, not the conductor, clerk, or oldest passenger. At 12:17, Mara watched an ordinary freight train pass without stopping. Afterward, she returned to her booth and noticed something new on the timetable. In tiny print beneath the mystery train was her own name, followed by tomorrow’s date.
