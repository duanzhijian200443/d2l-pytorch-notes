When Mara opened her little bakery, her grandson painted a sign on a leftover plank:

**FRESH BREAD AND A WARM WELLCOME**

“Oh,” he said, noticing the extra L after the paint had dried.

“Leave it,” Mara said. “Perhaps we have extra welcome.”

A cyclist photographed the sign. Soon, people from neighboring streets came to see it—and stayed for cinnamon buns. The newspaper called the bakery “the place with two Ls and plenty of heart.”

By autumn, someone offered Mara a proper printed sign.

She thanked them, but shook her head.

Every morning, her grandson still carried the crooked plank outside. And every morning, Mara set another chair beside the window, just in case the extra welcome needed somewhere to sit.
