Requiring reservations helps a popular community workshop manage capacity, staffing, materials, and safety. Participants gain certainty that a space is available, while organizers can communicate updates and plan sessions efficiently. However, reservations may disadvantage people with limited internet access, unpredictable schedules, or less familiarity with registration systems. No-shows can also leave seats unused, reducing access for others. A balanced approach could reserve most places while keeping some walk-in spots, offering phone registration, and using reminders or waitlists to improve fairness and attendance.
