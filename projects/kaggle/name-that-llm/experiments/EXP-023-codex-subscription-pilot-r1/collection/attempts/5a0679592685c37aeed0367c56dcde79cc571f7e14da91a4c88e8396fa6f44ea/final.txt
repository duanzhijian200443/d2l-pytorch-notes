A garden built around a strict visual design offers order, balance, and predictable seasonal displays. Carefully placed plants, defined borders, and repeated colors can create a polished, intentional space, but maintaining it often requires pruning, dividing, and replacing plants. Allowing plants to spread naturally produces a looser, evolving garden that supports wildlife and adapts to local conditions. It may need less intervention, yet vigorous species can dominate. The best choice depends on whether you value control and composition or spontaneity and ecological resilience.
