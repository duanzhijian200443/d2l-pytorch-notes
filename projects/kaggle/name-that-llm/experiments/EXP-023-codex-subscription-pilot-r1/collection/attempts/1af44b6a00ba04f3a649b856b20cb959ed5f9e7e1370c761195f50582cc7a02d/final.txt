Create a brief “closing commute.” Save your work, write tomorrow’s top three tasks, and shut the laptop completely. Put work items into a box, drawer, or bag so the table visibly changes. Wipe the surface, switch off the task light, and change the room’s lighting or music. Then leave the area for five minutes—take a short walk, stretch, or wash your hands. Return with a drink or place setting, signaling that the table is now for home life.
