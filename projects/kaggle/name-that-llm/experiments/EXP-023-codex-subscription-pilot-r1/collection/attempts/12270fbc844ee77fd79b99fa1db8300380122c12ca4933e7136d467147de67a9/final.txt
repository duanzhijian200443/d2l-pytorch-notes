Try a quick “pick three, choose one” system. Rotate who nominates three movies each movie night, encouraging genre mashups that appeal to several people, such as an animated adventure or romantic mystery. Everyone gets one veto, then randomly select from the remaining options. Set a five-minute deadline so choosing doesn’t become the main event. If all three are vetoed, use a pre-agreed fallback. Keep a shared watchlist, and rotate the nominator so everyone’s tastes get a fair turn.
