To scale the recipe from four people to six, first find the scaling factor by dividing the new number of servings by the original number:

6 ÷ 4 = 1.5

This means you need one and a half times as much of every ingredient. Since the original recipe uses three cups of broth, multiply that amount by 1.5:

3 × 1.5 = 4.5 cups

So, you need **4½ cups of broth for six people**.

Another way to check is to calculate the broth per person: three cups divided among four people is ¾ cup each. For six people, 6 × ¾ equals 4½ cups. Scaling all ingredients by the same factor keeps their proportions—and the recipe’s intended balance of flavors—the same.
