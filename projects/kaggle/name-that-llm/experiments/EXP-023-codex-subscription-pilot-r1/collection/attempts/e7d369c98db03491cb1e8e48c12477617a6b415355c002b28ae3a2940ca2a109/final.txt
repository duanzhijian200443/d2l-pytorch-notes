“Why is that square bare?” a visitor asked.  
“Does nothing grow there?”  
“Plenty might,” said the gardener.  
“Then why leave it?”  
“Because beneath it rests a little grave.”  
“For whom?”  
“A robin that sang here every dawn.”  
“Did you bury it?”  
“My daughter did. She asked that nothing disturb its sleep.”  
“So this empty patch is a garden too?”  
“Yes,” said the gardener. “It grows remembrance.”
