Every Thursday, Mara brought her father’s kitchen clock to Daniel’s shop. It lost four minutes a week.

Daniel replaced the spring, cleaned the gears, and had it keeping perfect time by noon.

When Mara returned, she mentioned that her father still made tea at six, waiting for his wife, who had died in March.

“She was always four minutes late,” Mara said.

After she left, Daniel reopened the clock. He adjusted it carefully, giving the old man a little longer to expect her.
