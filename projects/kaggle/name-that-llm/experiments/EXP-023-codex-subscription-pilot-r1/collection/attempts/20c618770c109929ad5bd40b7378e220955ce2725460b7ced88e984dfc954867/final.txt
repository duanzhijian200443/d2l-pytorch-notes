**Repair Afternoon**

Bring along a small broken household item and see whether our volunteer repairers can help bring it back to life. Join us this Sunday from **2:00 to 5:00 p.m.** at the **Community Hall**.

Volunteers will examine each item, explain what they are trying, and share practical repair tips. Please note that repairs cannot be guaranteed, so bring an item you’re prepared to leave unrepaired if it proves too difficult or parts are unavailable.

Enjoy a cup of tea while you wait and chat with fellow repair enthusiasts. **No advance booking is required**—just turn up with your broken household item and a willingness to learn.
