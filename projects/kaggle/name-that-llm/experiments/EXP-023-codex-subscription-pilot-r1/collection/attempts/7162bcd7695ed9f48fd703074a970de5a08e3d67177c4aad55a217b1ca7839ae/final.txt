Quiet seating offers space for relaxation, reading, and conversation, especially for older visitors and people seeking relief from noise. Active games encourage exercise, social interaction, and outdoor play, particularly for children and teenagers. Neither should dominate automatically: the best balance depends on local needs and existing facilities. A practical compromise is to create separate quiet and active zones, using trees or landscaping as buffers. Flexible lawns could accommodate games at designated times while remaining available for peaceful use otherwise.
