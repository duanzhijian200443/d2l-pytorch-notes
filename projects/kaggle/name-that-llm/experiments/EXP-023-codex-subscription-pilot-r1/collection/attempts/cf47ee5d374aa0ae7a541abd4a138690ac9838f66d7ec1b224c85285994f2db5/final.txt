Exact preservation protects authenticity, craftsmanship, and historical evidence. It lets visitors encounter a building much as earlier generations did, but may leave it underused, costly to maintain, or inaccessible.

Adaptation for public use can give the building a sustainable future—through galleries, housing, libraries, or community spaces—while expanding access and funding upkeep. However, new services, safety standards, and alterations can dilute original character. The best approach usually retains significant fabric while making carefully reversible, clearly contemporary changes.
