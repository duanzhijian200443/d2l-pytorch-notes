Requiring reservations helps a popular community workshop manage capacity, reduce overcrowding, and ensure participants have adequate tools, space, and instructor attention. It also makes staffing and material planning more predictable, while giving attendees confidence that they have a place. However, reservations can create barriers for people with unpredictable schedules or limited internet access. No-shows may waste scarce spots, and a formal system can make the workshop feel less spontaneous or welcoming. A fair cancellation policy and some walk-in spaces can balance access and efficiency.
