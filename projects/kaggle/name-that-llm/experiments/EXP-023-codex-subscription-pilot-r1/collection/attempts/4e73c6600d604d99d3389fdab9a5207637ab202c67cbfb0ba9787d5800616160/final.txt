Surveying only those who stayed until the end creates **selection (survivorship) bias**. Visitors who found the event too long may have left early and therefore had no chance to respond. The remaining participants are more likely to be especially interested, satisfied, or comfortable with the event’s length. As a result, the survey will probably overstate how many visitors thought the duration was appropriate and underrepresent complaints that it was too long. A representative survey should also include people who departed early.
