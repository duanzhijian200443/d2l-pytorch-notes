Mara returned the borrowed ladder just before sunset, carrying it across the alley to Mr. Vale’s blue gate. He thanked her, then frowned at the streak of silver paint on its highest rung.

“I found something on your roof,” she admitted.

From her pocket, Mara produced a weathered tin box. Inside lay old photographs of the neighborhood, including one of her grandmother dancing beside Mr. Vale beneath strings of lanterns.

He studied it quietly. “We held a rooftop party every spring,” he said. “Until everyone moved away.”

Across the alley, windows glowed one by one. Mr. Vale smiled. “I’m bringing the party back tonight. Music, lanterns, too much cake. Would you come?”

Mara looked at the photograph, then at the brightening rooftop.

“I’d love to.”
