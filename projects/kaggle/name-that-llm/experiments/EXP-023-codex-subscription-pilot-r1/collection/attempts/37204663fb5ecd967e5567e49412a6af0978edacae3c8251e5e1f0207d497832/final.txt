The neighborhood reading room should trial late opening on two evenings a week for one month, rather than stretching its limited volunteers across every evening. A dedicated quiet corner would support readers who need a calm space, while a simple noticeboard could share events, updates, and volunteering opportunities. During the trial, track attendance, gather visitor feedback, and check whether volunteers find the schedule manageable. At the end of the month, use those findings to decide whether to continue, adjust, or reduce the extended hours.
