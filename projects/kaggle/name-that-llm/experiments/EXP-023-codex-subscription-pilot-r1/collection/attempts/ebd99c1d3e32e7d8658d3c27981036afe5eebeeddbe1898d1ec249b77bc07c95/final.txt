Empty and wash everything first, then let it dry completely. Spread containers and lids across a large table. Group containers by shape and size, and test lids immediately; discard or recycle cracked, warped, stained, or permanently unmatched pieces. Keep only a practical number—perhaps enough for one week of leftovers—and donate usable extras. Store each container with its lid snapped on, or stack containers together and place lids vertically beside them in a small organizer. Label shelves or bins by size so matching pieces are easy to find. To prevent buildup, adopt a “one in, one out” rule: whenever a new container enters, remove an old one. After each use, return pieces to their designated spot and periodically check for damaged or orphaned items.
