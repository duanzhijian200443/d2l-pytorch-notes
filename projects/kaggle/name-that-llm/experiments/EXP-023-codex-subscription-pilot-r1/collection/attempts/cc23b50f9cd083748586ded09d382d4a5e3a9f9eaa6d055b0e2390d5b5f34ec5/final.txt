At the village fair, the inventor set a cardboard box on the table, threaded with loops of string. She placed two cups at opposite ends of the square, then asked a child to stand beside one and her grandfather beside the other. When the child spoke softly into his cup, the old man smiled: her words travelled along the taut string and arrived clearly at his ear.

Next, she pulled one cord, and a cardboard flap lifted a small basket from the floor to the tabletop. Another cord rang a bell at the gate. “It costs almost nothing,” she explained, “needs no batteries, and can call for help, carry a note, or raise supplies.” The judges watched the children immediately begin imagining strings between every cottage.
