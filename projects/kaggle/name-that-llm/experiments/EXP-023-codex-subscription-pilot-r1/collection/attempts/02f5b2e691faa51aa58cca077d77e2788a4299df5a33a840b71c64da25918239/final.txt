Make room for people, not a perfect setup. Clear one surface for food, borrow a few folding chairs, and let mismatched plates do their job. Cook one big, forgiving dish—a stew or baked pasta—with bread and a salad, so you’re not stuck in the kitchen. Put drinks where guests can help themselves. Keep music low and lighting warm. If someone offers to bring dessert or wash up, say yes. Your ease will set the mood more than any decorations.
