Create three clearly marked shoe zones—one per roommate—along a single wall, using painter’s tape. Limit each person to two everyday pairs in the entryway; store all others in bedrooms. Place shoes heel-to-wall and toe-out so they take less walking space. Keep a taped “door clearance” area completely empty, wide enough for the door to swing freely. Add a small shared basket or reusable bag for wet shoes, then move them once dry. Agree on a quick nightly reset.
