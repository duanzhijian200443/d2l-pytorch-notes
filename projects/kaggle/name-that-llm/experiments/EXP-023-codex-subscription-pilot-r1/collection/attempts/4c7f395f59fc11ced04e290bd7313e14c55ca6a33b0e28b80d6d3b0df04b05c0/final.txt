**Library Program Update: Confirmed Schedule Change and Proposed Addition**

Beginning next month, the library’s storytelling hour will officially move from its current weekday time to **Saturday**. This schedule change is confirmed, so families and participants should plan to attend the program on Saturdays starting next month. Specific times and dates will be shared through the library calendar and regular notices.

The library is also considering adding a **craft session immediately after storytelling hour**. This is only a proposal at this stage and has not yet been confirmed. Whether the craft session can begin will depend on having enough volunteers to help prepare materials, guide activities, and assist with cleanup. Anyone interested in volunteering is encouraged to contact the library. A separate announcement will be made if the session receives sufficient support and is approved.

The existing **weekday book exchange will continue without any changes**. Visitors may keep using that service on its current day and schedule.
