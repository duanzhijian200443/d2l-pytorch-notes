Divide work by function, workload, and decision rights—not just equal task counts. One collaborator can lead editorial work: gathering stories, writing, editing, and maintaining the publishing calendar. A second can manage community outreach: soliciting submissions, coordinating contributors, and responding to readers. The third can own operations: layout, email-platform setup, analytics, budget, and distribution.

Rotate visible or high-effort duties periodically, such as leading meetings, writing the editor’s note, or handling urgent inbox messages. Agree on expected weekly hours and document who owns each recurring task. Major decisions—mission, publication frequency, budget, and sensitive editorial choices—should require joint discussion. Hold a brief weekly check-in to rebalance work when one role becomes heavier than expected.
