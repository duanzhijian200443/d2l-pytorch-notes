A strict visual garden design emphasizes order, symmetry, and predictable color or height schemes. It suits formal spaces and makes maintenance, paths, and focal points easier to manage. Allowing plants to spread naturally creates a looser, more ecological garden, often supporting wildlife and reducing the need for constant intervention. However, it can look untidy or let vigorous species dominate. A balanced approach uses deliberate structure—beds, borders, and anchors—while leaving room for self-seeding and gentle natural change.
