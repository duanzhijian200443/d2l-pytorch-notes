Public parks should serve both relaxation and recreation. Quiet seating supports older adults, readers, caregivers, and anyone seeking refuge from urban noise. Active-game areas encourage exercise, social connection, and youth engagement, making the park lively and inclusive. A practical compromise is to zone the park: place courts and play fields near entrances, buffered by trees or landscaping, while creating shaded, accessible quiet gardens farther away. Flexible lawns can host games at set times and remain peaceful otherwise.
