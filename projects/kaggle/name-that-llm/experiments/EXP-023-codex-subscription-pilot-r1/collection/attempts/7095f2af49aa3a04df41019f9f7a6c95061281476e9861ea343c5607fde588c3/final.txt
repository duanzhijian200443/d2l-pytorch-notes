At dawn, the ferry slips across grey water, its engines humming softly beneath the hush of the morning. A paper bag rests warm in my hands, releasing the comforting scent of fresh bread. Nearby, a child counts the buoys as they rise and vanish behind us, keeping careful track of the crossing. For a long while, the far shore is only a pale suggestion through the mist; then, almost imperceptibly, its shapes gather and become distinct.
