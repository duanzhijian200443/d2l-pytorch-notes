## Do You Remember the Old Swimming Pool?

We’re recording residents’ memories of the old swimming pool and would love to hear yours. Whether you remember learning to swim, visiting with friends, or spending time there with family, your recollections could help bring its story to life.

Interviews take around twenty minutes. You don’t need a prepared speech or a remarkable story—everyday memories are welcome, too. You might recall a familiar face, a favourite moment, or simply what the pool was like when you visited.

Taking part is entirely optional. If you decide to contribute, you can choose whether your name appears alongside your memories.

Edited extracts from the interviews will be displayed at the community center, giving others a chance to discover and reflect on residents’ experiences of the pool.

If you have a memory to share, please consider joining in. Your voice could help celebrate a place that was part of local life.
