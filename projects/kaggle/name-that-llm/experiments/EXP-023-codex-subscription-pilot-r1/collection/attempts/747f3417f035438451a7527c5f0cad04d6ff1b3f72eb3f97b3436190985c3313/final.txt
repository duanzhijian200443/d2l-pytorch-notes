Sort the postcards and ticket stubs into a few loose themes—trips, concerts, friends, or years—without trying to preserve everything. Choose 20–40 favorites and place them in a simple archival photo box or a small binder with clear pockets. Add brief pencil notes on removable labels: date, place, and why it matters.

For a little personality, make one “highlight page” per theme using a sheet of cardstock and a few photo corners—no gluing required. Keep duplicates and less meaningful pieces in an envelope inside the box. The goal is a collection you can browse easily, not a perfect scrapbook.
