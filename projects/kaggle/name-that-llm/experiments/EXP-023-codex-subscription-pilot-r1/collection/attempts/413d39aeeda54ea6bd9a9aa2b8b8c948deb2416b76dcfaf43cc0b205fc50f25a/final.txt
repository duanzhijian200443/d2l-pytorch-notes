**Friendly Reminder for Volunteers**

Thank you for helping make our event a success! Before you leave, please remember to return any borrowed name badges and room keys to the volunteer check-in desk or the designated event coordinator.

Returning these items promptly helps us keep materials organized, protects access to event spaces, and ensures everything is ready for future programs. Please do not take badges or keys home, even if you plan to volunteer again soon. If you have already left the venue and accidentally still have an item, please contact the event organizer as soon as possible to arrange its return.

We truly appreciate your time, energy, and cooperation. Thank you again for volunteering and for helping us wrap up the event smoothly!
