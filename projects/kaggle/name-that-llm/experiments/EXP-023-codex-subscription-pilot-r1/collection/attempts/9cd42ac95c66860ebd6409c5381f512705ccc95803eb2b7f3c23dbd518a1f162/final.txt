Empty the cupboard and place every container and lid on a large table or floor. Discard cracked, warped, stained, or lidless pieces you no longer need. Group containers by shape and size, then match each one with a lid. Test every pairing; recycle or donate unmatched items if your local options allow. Keep only a practical number of complete sets, preferably models that nest and share lid sizes.

To prevent another buildup, store containers nested together and lids upright in a divider or small bin, organized by size. Avoid keeping disposable takeout tubs unless they have a specific use. When buying replacements, choose the same brand or standardized set. Adopt a simple rule: no container enters storage without a matching lid, and periodically remove damaged or unmatched pieces.
