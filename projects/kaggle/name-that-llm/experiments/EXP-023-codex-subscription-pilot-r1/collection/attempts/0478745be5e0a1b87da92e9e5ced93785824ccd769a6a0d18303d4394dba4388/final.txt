At six, Mrs. Vale came in for a spool of blue thread.

“Still mending that coat?” Arun asked.

“It’s a good coat.”

He found the shade without checking the drawer labels. Around them, empty shelves showed pale rectangles where boxes had stood for forty-three years.

She counted out the coins. He wrapped the spool in tissue, as his father had taught him.

At the door, she turned.

“See you Monday.”

Neither corrected her.

Arun waited until she reached the corner before switching off the light.
