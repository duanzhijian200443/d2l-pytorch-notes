Mara noticed the empty stake beside the tomato bed just as she was watering. Its label had vanished, leaving only a pale rectangle of cleaner wood.

“Do you know what was planted here?” she asked the man tending sunflowers nearby.

He smiled. “I was hoping you’d ask. I planted it, then forgot.”

Together they inspected the leaves, argued gently over basil versus pepper, and asked passing gardeners for opinions. By noon, they had agreed it was probably a rare heirloom chili—and that neither of them had much experience growing one.

The man introduced himself as Luis. Mara offered to bring her grandmother’s pepper stew recipe if the plant survived.

By autumn, the label read: **Luis and Mara’s Mystery Chili.**
