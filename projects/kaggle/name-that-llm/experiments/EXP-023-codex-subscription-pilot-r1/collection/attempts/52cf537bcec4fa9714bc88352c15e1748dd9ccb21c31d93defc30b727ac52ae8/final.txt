Divide responsibility by assigning each collaborator a clear area of ownership while sharing major decisions. For example, one person can manage editorial planning, writing, and fact-checking; another can handle design, formatting, and publication; and the third can coordinate community outreach, subscriptions, and feedback. Set expectations together, including deadlines, quality standards, and how much time each person can contribute. Rotate less popular tasks, such as proofreading or administrative work, so the burden stays fair. Use a shared calendar and task list to make work visible, and hold brief regular check-ins. Decisions affecting the newsletter’s mission, budget, or public voice should require agreement from all three. Revisit the arrangement after the first few issues and adjust responsibilities based on actual workload and strengths.
