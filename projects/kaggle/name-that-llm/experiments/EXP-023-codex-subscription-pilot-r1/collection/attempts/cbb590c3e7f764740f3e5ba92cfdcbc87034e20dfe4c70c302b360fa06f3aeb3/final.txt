A few shared conventions can make the calendar easier to scan and act on:

- **Use descriptive titles:** Replace “thing” with “Budget review — Finance” and “appointment” with “Dentist — Alex.”
- **Identify who’s involved:** Add an owner or relevant team, and invite only people who need to attend.
- **Include practical details:** Put the location or meeting link, purpose, and any preparation in the description.
- **Keep timing accurate:** Set realistic start and end times; use all-day entries only when appropriate.
- **Label consistently:** Agree on a few categories, such as meetings, deadlines, leave, and personal commitments.
- **Maintain it:** Update changes promptly and delete canceled or obsolete recurring events.

For private commitments, “Unavailable — Alex” provides useful scheduling information without revealing sensitive details.
