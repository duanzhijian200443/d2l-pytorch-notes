Mara set a cardboard box on the village hall table and handed the mayor a length of string.

“Pull.”

He did. Inside the box, a cardboard wheel turned, lifting a tiny gate. A potato rolled down a sloping channel and dropped into a basket.

The audience waited.

“My father can’t bend his knees,” Mara explained. “This fits beside his chair. Potatoes go in the top. He pulls the string, gets one at a time, and peels them.”

“Only potatoes?” asked the baker.

Mara tipped in three onions.

By the time the mayor had dispensed an apple, a spool of thread, and his own spectacles, everyone was standing.

Her father stayed seated. He pulled the string and caught the next potato, smiling.
