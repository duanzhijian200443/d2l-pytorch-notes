The shop should compare the benefits of variety with the risk of stockouts. More postcard designs may attract visitors with different tastes, encourage browsing, and showcase more exhibits, but each design may sell slowly and create leftover inventory. Deeper quantities of popular cards reduce missed sales and simplify purchasing, yet may disappoint customers seeking something distinctive. The shop could analyze sales by design, profit margins, seasonality, and storage costs, then reserve most stock for proven sellers while testing new designs in small batches.
