### Repair Afternoon Reminder

Join us this **Sunday from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. at the Community Hall** for a friendly repair afternoon. Bring along **one small broken household item**, and our volunteers will take a look and see whether they can help give it a new life.

Please note that **repairs cannot be guaranteed**, but volunteers will explain what they are trying, so you may learn useful repair skills along the way. It is a relaxed opportunity to reduce waste, meet others in the community, and discover how everyday objects work.

**Tea will be available**, so you can enjoy a drink while you wait. There is **no need to book in advance**—just come along during the afternoon with your item.
