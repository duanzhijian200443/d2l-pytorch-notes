An error log can reveal what failed, when it failed, and where the application noticed the problem. It may include exception types, stack traces, failed requests, dependency errors, and contextual details such as user actions or resource usage. Repeated patterns can help identify affected components, estimate frequency, and suggest likely causes.

However, a log usually records symptoms rather than proving the root cause. The reported location may be where an earlier problem surfaced, not where it began. Logs alone generally cannot establish the full sequence of events, how many users were affected, whether missing events occurred, or whether a proposed fix works. Reliable diagnosis typically requires correlating logs with metrics, traces, configuration, recent changes, and reproducible tests.
