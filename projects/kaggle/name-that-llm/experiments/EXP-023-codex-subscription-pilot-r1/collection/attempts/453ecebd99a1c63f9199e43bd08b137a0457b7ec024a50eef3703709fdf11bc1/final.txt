Every morning, Mara repaired the clocks in the old railway station. One belonged to Mr. Vale, a widower who arrived early each day to meet the train his wife had once taken home. Mara could have set it perfectly, but left it seven minutes slow—the same seven minutes he used to sit quietly on the bench before the whistle, gathering courage for another day. When he thanked her, she only smiled. Some clocks measured time; this one made room for it.
