At dawn, the ferry eased across the grey water, its engine humming beneath the quiet of the morning. I held a paper bag of warm bread, breathing in its comforting smell as the wind sharpened around us. Nearby, a child counted each buoy aloud, losing track and beginning again with renewed determination. For a long while, the far shore was only a smudge in the mist. Then, almost imperceptibly, edges appeared, colours deepened, and our destination slowly became real.
