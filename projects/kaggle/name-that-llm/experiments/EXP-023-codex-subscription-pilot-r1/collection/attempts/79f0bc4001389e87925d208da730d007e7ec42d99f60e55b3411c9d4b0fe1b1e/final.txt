Make a small “places and moments” box rather than a scrapbook. Pick a shoebox-sized photo box and set a 30-minute timer.

- Choose 20–30 postcards and ticket stubs that bring back a specific memory. You don’t need to represent every trip.
- Sort them into a few loose groups: trips, performances, everyday adventures.
- Slip fragile pieces into archival sleeves, or place them between acid-free cards.
- Add a brief pencil note on a separate card: who you were with, something funny, or why you kept it.
- Use simple index-card dividers—no decorating required.

Keep the box somewhere accessible so you can occasionally browse it. Put undecided items in one envelope and revisit them later; you don’t have to make every decision today.
