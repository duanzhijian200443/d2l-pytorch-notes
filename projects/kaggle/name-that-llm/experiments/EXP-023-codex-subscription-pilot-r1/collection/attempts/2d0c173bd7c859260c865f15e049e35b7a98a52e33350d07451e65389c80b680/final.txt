The library’s storytelling hour is moving to Saturday next month. This schedule change is confirmed, so please plan to join us on Saturdays rather than at the current storytelling time once the new month begins.

We are also considering adding a craft session immediately after storytelling. This is a proposal, not a confirmed addition to the library’s program. Whether it goes ahead will depend on having enough volunteers to help run it. For now, please do not assume that a craft session will be available when the Saturday storytelling schedule starts. If you are interested in volunteering, please let the library know.

The current weekday book exchange will continue as usual. Its schedule is not affected by the move to Saturday storytelling or by the proposed craft session.

In short: Saturday storytelling is confirmed for next month; an afterward craft session remains a possibility, subject to volunteer support; and the weekday book exchange is unchanged.
