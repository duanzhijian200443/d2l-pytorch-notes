Your browser saves copies of website files—images, styles, scripts, and sometimes pages—to load sites faster. After a website update, it may still show those saved copies until the cache expires or is refreshed. Try a hard refresh (often Ctrl+F5 on Windows or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac), open the site in a private window, or clear the browser cache. If only your laptop shows the old version, caching is likely the reason.
