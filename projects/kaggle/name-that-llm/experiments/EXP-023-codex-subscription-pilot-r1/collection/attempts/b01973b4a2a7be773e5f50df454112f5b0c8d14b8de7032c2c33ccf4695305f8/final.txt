Treat an art museum less like a test and more like a place to wander. You do not need to understand every painting, recognize famous artists, or read every label. Start with a short visit—perhaps an hour—and follow whatever catches your eye. Pause when a color, face, texture, or strange detail interests you, even if you cannot explain why.

Try choosing just a few works to spend time with. Ask simple questions: What do I notice first? What mood does this create? What story might be happening? There are no wrong personal reactions. Read a label only when you are curious for more context; it is an optional tool, not an assignment.

You might also pick a playful theme, such as finding the funniest expression, the best outfit, or one work you would take home. Sit down, sketch, or talk with a friend about what you like and dislike. Leave before you feel exhausted. Enjoyment and curiosity are enough; expertise can come later, if you want it.
