Try this simple two-week plan:

- **Start with what you have:** a window, a notebook or phone, and five minutes each morning. Binoculars aren’t necessary.
- **Notice features before guessing names:** size, main colors, beak shape, tail length, and behavior. Does it hop, walk, or cling to branches?
- **Learn three local birds first:** search a free regional bird guide or bird-identification app. Choose birds common in your town, rather than memorizing a whole country’s species.
- **Keep a tiny bird log:** date, description, likely name, and a quick sketch or photo if possible.
- **Repeat and compare:** revisit uncertain sightings. Listen for calls, too.
- **Add one new name every few days.** Check several features before deciding; “not sure yet” is useful progress.
