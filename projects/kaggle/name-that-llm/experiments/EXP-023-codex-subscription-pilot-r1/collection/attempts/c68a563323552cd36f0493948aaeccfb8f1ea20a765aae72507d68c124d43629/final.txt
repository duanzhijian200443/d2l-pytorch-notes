A casual walking group can accommodate different speeds by agreeing on a few simple practices. Start with a shared route that includes natural regrouping points, such as benches, crossings, viewpoints, or cafés. Encourage people to walk in small pace groups rather than forcing everyone to match one speed. The fastest walkers can loop back periodically, while slower walkers should never feel pressured to hurry.

To give everyone a chance to talk, occasionally change walking partners or use a buddy system for parts of the route. Keep some sections as “conversation pairs,” then regroup for broader discussion. The group might also schedule a short mid-walk break where everyone can chat together.

A designated leader or sweep walker can help ensure nobody is left behind, and the route should have an easy shorter option for anyone who wants it. Most importantly, present different speeds as normal rather than as a problem. The goal is social connection, not distance or pace, so the group should prioritize inclusion and comfort.
