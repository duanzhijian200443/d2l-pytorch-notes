A public park should balance quiet seating with space for active games. Quiet areas offer relaxation, reading, and peaceful contact with nature, benefiting older people, workers, and families seeking calm. Active spaces, however, promote exercise, social interaction, and healthy recreation for children and adults. A practical compromise would divide the park into clearly separated zones, using trees or low barriers to reduce noise. Flexible lawns could serve both purposes at different times, ensuring the park remains welcoming, lively, and peaceful for everyone.
