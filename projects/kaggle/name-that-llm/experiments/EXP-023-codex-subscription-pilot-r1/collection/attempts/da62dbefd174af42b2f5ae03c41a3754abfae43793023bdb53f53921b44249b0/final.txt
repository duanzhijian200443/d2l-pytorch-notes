Try a quick “ranked shortlist.” Each person suggests one movie from their favorite genre, then everyone privately ranks the options. Choose the film with the best overall ranking—not simply the most first-place votes. Set a five-minute limit for nominations and voting to keep the evening moving. To make the result feel fair, rotate who breaks ties each movie night, or let the person whose choice ranks lowest this time pick first next time. This balances preferences without turning entertainment into a lengthy negotiation.
