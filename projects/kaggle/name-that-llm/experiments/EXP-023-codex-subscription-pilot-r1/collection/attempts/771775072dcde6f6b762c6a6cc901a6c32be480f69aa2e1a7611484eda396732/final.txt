One late trip is not enough to judge the route’s overall reliability. Useful observations would include arrival times across many trips, on different days and at different times, compared with the published schedule. Note how often buses are late, by how many minutes, and whether delays cluster during rush hour, bad weather, roadworks, or special events. It would also help to compare the route with similar routes and distinguish occasional exceptional delays from a consistent pattern. Passenger reports and transit-operator performance data could provide further evidence.
