Providing identical materials promotes fairness because everyone begins with the same resources, regardless of income or access. It also makes the workshop easier to organize: instructors know what tools are available, can plan suitable activities, and can help participants more efficiently. However, standardized materials may limit creativity if everyone is encouraged toward similar results.

Allowing participants to bring whatever they have can inspire originality. Different tools, textures, and objects may lead to unexpected ideas and encourage resourcefulness. It is also convenient for organizers because they need not purchase everything. Yet this approach may be less convenient for participants and could feel unfair, since some people may bring expensive or specialized supplies while others have very little. A balanced solution is to provide essential materials equally while allowing optional personal items.
