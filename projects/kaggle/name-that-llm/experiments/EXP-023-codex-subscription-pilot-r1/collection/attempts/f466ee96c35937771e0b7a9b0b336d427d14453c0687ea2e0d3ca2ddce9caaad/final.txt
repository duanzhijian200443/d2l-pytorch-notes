A pilot staffed by enthusiastic volunteers benefits from unusually high motivation, patience, skill, and willingness to tolerate problems. Participants may also receive extra training, attention, and support because the project is small and visible. A full rollout includes people with varied abilities, priorities, incentives, and resistance to change, while support resources are spread more thinly. Processes that worked informally at pilot scale may not handle greater complexity or volume. Thus, pilot success may reflect favorable participants and conditions rather than a universally scalable solution.
