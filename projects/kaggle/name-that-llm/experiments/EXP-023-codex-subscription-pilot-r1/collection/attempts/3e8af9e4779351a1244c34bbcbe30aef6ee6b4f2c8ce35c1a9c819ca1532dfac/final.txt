Welcome! New members are invited to attend their first session free of charge—a great opportunity to meet the group and explore the workspace. Tools are shared among participants, so we ask everyone to use them thoughtfully and return them promptly for others. Please plan to take your project home at the end of each session, as we’re unable to store unfinished work between meetings. Our instructor is happy to answer questions, explain techniques, and help you work through challenges. However, they won’t complete projects on participants’ behalf. We want everyone to enjoy learning, build confidence, and leave with work that truly reflects their own creativity and effort. We look forward to welcoming you!
