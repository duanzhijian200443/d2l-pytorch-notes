Keep the menu simple: one make-ahead main, a big salad, bread, and a dessert that needs no last-minute fuss. Serve everything family-style and let guests help themselves. Clear unnecessary furniture and coats, but don’t chase perfection—a little clutter feels lived-in. Use soft lighting, open a window briefly before people arrive, and play a low-key playlist. Mix seating if needed; stools and cushions are fine. Most importantly, finish cooking early so you can greet everyone with a drink and actually join the evening.
