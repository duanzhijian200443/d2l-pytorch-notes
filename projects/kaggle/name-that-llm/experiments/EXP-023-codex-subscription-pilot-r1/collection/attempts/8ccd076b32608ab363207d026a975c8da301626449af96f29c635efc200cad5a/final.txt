Run a low-cost two-week demand test before redesigning the menu. Keep the popular item, but offer small, clearly labeled batches of the leftover items at different times, prices, or in sampler boxes. Track production quantities, sales by hour, customer comments, and items customers ask for but cannot buy. Add a brief QR-code survey or ask checkout questions about preferred flavors and sizes. Compare results by day, then adjust baking quantities and promotions gradually rather than removing products immediately.
