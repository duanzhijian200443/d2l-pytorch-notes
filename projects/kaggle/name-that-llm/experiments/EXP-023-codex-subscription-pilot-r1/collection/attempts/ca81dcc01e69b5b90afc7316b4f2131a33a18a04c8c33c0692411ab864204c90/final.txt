Use a consistent naming format so entries are understandable at a glance:

- **Start with the purpose:** “Dentist – Alex” or “Project review – Website,” rather than “appointment” or “meeting.”
- **Include the person responsible:** Add a name or initials when ownership is not obvious.
- **Add location or meeting link:** Put these in the location field, not only in notes.
- **Use agreed colors or separate calendars:** For example, work, family, school, travel, and deadlines.
- **Mark availability accurately:** Distinguish “busy,” “tentative,” and informational events.
- **Include useful details:** Add preparation notes, booking references, contacts, or required documents in the description.
- **Set realistic start and end times:** Include travel or setup time when relevant.
- **Avoid unnecessary duplication:** Assign one organizer and update the existing event when plans change.
