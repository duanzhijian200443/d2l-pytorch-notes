Try a **“keep, pause, release” reset**—not a test of whether you’re disciplined enough.

Gather your supplies by hobby. For each one, ask:
- If I hadn’t already spent money on this, would I choose it today?
- Do I enjoy doing it, or mainly imagine enjoying the finished result?
- Does it fit my current time, energy, and space?

Then sort:

**Keep:** You genuinely want to do it soon. Make supplies accessible and choose one tiny next step, like sketching for ten minutes.

**Pause:** You’re interested, but this isn’t the season. Put supplies in a labeled box with a review date three months away. Store it out of sight; pausing doesn’t require daily guilt.

**Give away:** The hobby feels mostly like obligation. Offer usable supplies to a friend, school, or community group. You can keep one meaningful item without keeping everything.

The money bought an opportunity to explore—not a lifelong assignment. Learning that something isn’t for you is a valid outcome.
