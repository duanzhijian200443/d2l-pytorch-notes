“Why is that little square bare?” the visitor asked.

“So the ground can breathe,” said the gardener.

“Doesn’t the rest breathe beneath the flowers?”

“Not in the same way.”

“What are you waiting for—rain?”

“Footprints.”

“Whose?”

“Beetles, sparrows, perhaps a hedgehog.”

“And if none come?”

“Then I’ll know the garden is beautiful but lonely.”

The visitor studied the plain brown patch.

“So it’s a door?”

The gardener smiled. “Yes. Every garden should leave one door unlocked.”
