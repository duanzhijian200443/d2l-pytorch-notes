A casual walking group can accommodate different paces by making flexibility part of the plan rather than expecting everyone to walk together constantly.

Choose routes with wide paths, clear landmarks, and convenient meeting points. Start with five to ten minutes at an easy pace so everyone can greet one another, then let people naturally form smaller groups based on their preferred speed. Agree beforehand on regrouping spots, such as a bench, viewpoint, or café, so nobody feels abandoned or pressured to keep up.

To encourage conversation across the group, invite people to switch walking partners after each stop. Faster walkers can occasionally choose a slower stretch to chat, but nobody should feel obligated to walk at an uncomfortable pace. Optional loops can give brisk walkers extra distance while others take a shorter route or rest.

Finish with a shared cooldown or refreshments. Emphasize that the aim is enjoyable movement and connection, not matching speeds, and check periodically that the arrangement works for everyone.
