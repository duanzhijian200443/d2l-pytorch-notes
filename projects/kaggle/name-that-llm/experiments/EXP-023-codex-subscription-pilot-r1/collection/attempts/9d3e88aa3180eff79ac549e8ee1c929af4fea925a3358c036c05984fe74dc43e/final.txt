**Library Schedule Update**

We’re pleased to confirm that the library’s weekly storytelling hour will move to **Saturdays beginning next month**. The new schedule is intended to make the program more accessible to families and community members who cannot attend during the week. Please watch for the exact Saturday start time, which will be announced soon.

We are also considering a **possible craft session after storytelling hour**. This is still a proposal, not a confirmed program. We will offer the session only if enough volunteers are available to help prepare materials, lead activities, and clean up afterward. If you would like to volunteer or share ideas for crafts, please contact the library desk. We will announce whether the craft session will launch once volunteer interest has been assessed.

Please note that the **weekday book exchange will continue as usual**. Its schedule and format are not changing. Thank you for supporting the library’s programs and helping us make these activities welcoming and sustainable for everyone.
