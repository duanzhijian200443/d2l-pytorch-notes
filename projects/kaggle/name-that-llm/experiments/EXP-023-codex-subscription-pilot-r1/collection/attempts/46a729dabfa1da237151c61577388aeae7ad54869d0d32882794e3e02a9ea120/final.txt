Dear Volunteers,

Thank you for the time, energy, and care you contributed to making the event a success. As we wrap up, please remember to return any borrowed name badges and room keys before leaving.

You may return them at the registration desk or to an event coordinator. Prompt returns help us keep our records accurate, prepare for future events, and avoid replacement costs.

If you have already returned your items, thank you—no further action is needed. If you are unable to return them today, please contact the volunteer coordinator as soon as possible to arrange a convenient time.

We truly appreciate your cooperation and all that you do.
