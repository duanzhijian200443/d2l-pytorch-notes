The spread of reliable postal services made distant relationships easier to sustain by turning occasional, uncertain contact into regular communication. Families separated by migration, military service, or work could exchange news, offer reassurance, and remain involved in one another’s lives. Friends and romantic partners could develop intimacy through letters, sharing thoughts and feelings despite physical separation. Predictable delivery also encouraged people to expect replies, creating routines that strengthened emotional bonds and mutual obligations. Cheaper, more accessible services extended these possibilities beyond wealthy elites, although literacy and cost still limited participation. Letters could not replace face-to-face contact, and delays or misunderstandings remained common. Nevertheless, dependable post made distance less likely to mean silence, allowing relationships to endure across regions and continents.
