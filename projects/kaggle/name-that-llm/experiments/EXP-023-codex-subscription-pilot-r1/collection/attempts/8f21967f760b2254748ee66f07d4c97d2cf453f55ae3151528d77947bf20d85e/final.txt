Dust sifted through the attic light as Mara labeled boxes: **KEEP**, **SELL**, **TRASH**.

Jonah held up a chipped blue cup. “This goes.”

Mara reached for it. “No.”

“It’s ugly.”

“So are half your shirts.”

He dropped the cup into **SELL**. She snatched it back, setting it carefully beside the window. Then she opened a box marked **MOM**, though neither had touched it all morning.

Inside lay a faded scarf and an old hospital bracelet. Mara wrapped the cup in the scarf. Jonah watched her, jaw tight.

“She used it every morning,” Mara said.

“She couldn’t lift it by the end.”

Mara’s hands froze.

Jonah took the cup, washed its dust away with his sleeve, and placed it in **KEEP**.

Neither sibling spoke. They began sorting the box together.
