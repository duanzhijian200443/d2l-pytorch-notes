Every morning, Mara announced the 7:10, the 7:26, and the delayed service to Preston. Then she found an old timetable beneath the ticket desk. Between familiar departures was the 8:03 to Bellweather. No colleague remembered it. Neither did the station manager.

At 8:03 the next day, Mara watched the empty platform. No train came. But when the clock clicked over, a loose timetable fluttered from the bench, revealing a small brass ticket stamped: **BELLWEATHER, RETURN**.
