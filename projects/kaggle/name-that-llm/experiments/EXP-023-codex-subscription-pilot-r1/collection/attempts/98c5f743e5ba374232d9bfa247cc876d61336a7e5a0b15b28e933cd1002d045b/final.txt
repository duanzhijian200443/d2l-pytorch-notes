I heard the bell as Mara slipped into my shop, rain dripping from her coat. She lingered over the little ceramic bird, turning it gently until her thumb found the crack. I thought she might ask for a discount, but she set it down and chose a postcard instead. After paying, she paused beside the bird and turned its unbroken side toward the door. Then she stepped back into the rain, leaving my shabby little display looking a little less forlorn.
