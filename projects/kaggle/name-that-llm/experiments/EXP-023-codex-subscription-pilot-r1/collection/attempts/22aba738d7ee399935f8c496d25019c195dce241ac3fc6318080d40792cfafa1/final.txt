The shop should compare sales data, visitor preferences, profit margins, and storage costs. More varieties may appeal to diverse tastes and showcase more of the museum’s collection, but slow-selling designs can tie up money and space. Deeper stocks of popular postcards reduce stockouts and may secure bulk discounts, though limited choice could disappoint visitors. A balanced approach is to maintain strong supplies of bestsellers while testing smaller quantities of new designs, then adjust the mix using sales results and customer feedback.
