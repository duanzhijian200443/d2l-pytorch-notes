Before copying a competitor’s feature, the team should understand whether it solves a real problem for *their* users. Learn:

- Who would use it, and which user segments matter most.
- The jobs users are trying to accomplish and current pain points.
- How users solve the problem today, including workarounds and competing products.
- Whether the competitor’s feature is actually driving adoption, retention, or satisfaction—or merely attracting attention.
- How the feature fits users’ existing workflows, expectations, and willingness to pay.
- What trade-offs it introduces: complexity, privacy concerns, learning cost, or distraction from higher-priority needs.
- What distinctive need their own product could address better.

Validate assumptions through interviews, support data, usage analytics, and small prototypes. Copying is worthwhile only if it advances a user-centered product strategy, not because the feature is fashionable.
