Mara announced the 6:12 to Brighton every weekday from Platform 3. One damp Tuesday, a faded timetable listed a 6:17 to Ashcombe. No clerk remembered it; no passenger had heard of Ashcombe.

At 6:17, no train came. Still, Mara made the announcement.

After her shift, she checked the old noticeboard behind Platform 3. Tucked beneath it was a child’s blue ticket, punched neatly: **ASHCOMBE — ONE WAY.**
