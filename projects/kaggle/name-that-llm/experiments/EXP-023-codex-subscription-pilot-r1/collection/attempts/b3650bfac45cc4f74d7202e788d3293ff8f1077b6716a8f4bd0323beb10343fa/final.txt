Canvas awnings cast uneven shade across the small market, where stalls crowd beneath the gate. Near one table, a woman quietly arranges pears by size, setting the smallest in neat rows beside the larger fruit. The smell of fresh coffee drifts from somewhere near the entrance, mixing with the scent of produce and damp canvas. At the edge of the lane, a vendor crouches beside a stall, tightening something around a loose wheel before it can wobble away again. Above the gate, the old clock marks the hour with misplaced confidence: it is ten minutes fast, as everyone who comes through seems to know.
