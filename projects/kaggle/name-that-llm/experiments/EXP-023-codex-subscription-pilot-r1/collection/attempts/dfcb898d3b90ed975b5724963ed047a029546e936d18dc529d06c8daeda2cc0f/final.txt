Try a quick “genre lottery.” Each family member nominates one movie, then everyone rates each option from 1–5 for enjoyment and suitability. Add the scores, but give a small bonus to movies that appeal across genres. Set a strict two-minute limit, and let the highest-scoring film win. To keep things fair, rotate who gets a bonus choice or who breaks ties each week. Save the other nominees for future movie nights rather than debating them endlessly.
