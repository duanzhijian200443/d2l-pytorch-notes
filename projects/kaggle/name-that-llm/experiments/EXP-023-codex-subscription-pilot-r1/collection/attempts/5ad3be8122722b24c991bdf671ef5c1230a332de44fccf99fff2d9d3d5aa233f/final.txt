Use a simple two-stage process: **curate, then select**.

First, gather photos by event or month and quickly delete obvious duplicates, blinks, technical failures, and near-identical shots. For each remaining group, keep the best one or two that tell the story: a strong expression, meaningful interaction, distinctive setting, or useful context. Avoid choosing only technically perfect images—emotional value matters more.

Next, create a temporary “print candidates” album. Limit yourself to a fixed number per event, perhaps 5–10, and review the album after a week. Ask: *Would I enjoy seeing this on a wall or in an album years from now? Does it represent someone or something important?* Print a balanced mix of people, places, details, and candid moments, rather than many variations of the same pose.
