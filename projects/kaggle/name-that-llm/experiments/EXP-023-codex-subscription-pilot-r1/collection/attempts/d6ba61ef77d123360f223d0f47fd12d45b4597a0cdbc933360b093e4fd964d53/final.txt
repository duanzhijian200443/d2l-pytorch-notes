The clocks start synchronized, so their readings initially differ by zero. Each day, the first clock gains 2 minutes relative to real time, while the second loses 1 minute. Therefore, the gap between them increases by:

2 − (−1) = 3 minutes per day.

After four days, the total difference is:

4 × 3 = 12 minutes.

Thus, the fast clock will read **12 minutes later** than the slow clock. For example, after exactly four real days (96 hours), the gaining clock will show 96 hours and 8 minutes, while the losing clock will show 95 hours and 56 minutes. The difference between those readings is 12 minutes.
