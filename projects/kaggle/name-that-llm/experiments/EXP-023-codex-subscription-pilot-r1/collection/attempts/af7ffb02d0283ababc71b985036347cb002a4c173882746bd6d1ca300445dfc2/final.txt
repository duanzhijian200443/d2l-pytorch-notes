Browser caching means your laptop saves copies of website files—such as images, stylesheets, and scripts—so pages load faster next time. After a website is updated, your browser may still display those older saved files, making the site appear unchanged. Try refreshing the page first. If that fails, use a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5 on Windows, or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac), clear the browser cache, or open the site in a private window. Sometimes a company proxy or CDN may also temporarily serve an older version.
