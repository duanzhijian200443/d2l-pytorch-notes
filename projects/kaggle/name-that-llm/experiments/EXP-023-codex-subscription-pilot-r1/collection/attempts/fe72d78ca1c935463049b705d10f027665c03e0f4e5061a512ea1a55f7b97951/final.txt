Choose more varieties if visitors have diverse tastes, seasonal interests, or seek unusual souvenirs; breadth can increase impulse purchases and represent more artworks. Choose deeper quantities of popular designs when sales data shows consistent demand, storage is limited, and stockouts lose revenue. The shop should review sales by design, margin, replenishment time, and visitor feedback. A balanced approach works well: maintain reliable stock of bestsellers while testing smaller quantities of new or niche postcards.
