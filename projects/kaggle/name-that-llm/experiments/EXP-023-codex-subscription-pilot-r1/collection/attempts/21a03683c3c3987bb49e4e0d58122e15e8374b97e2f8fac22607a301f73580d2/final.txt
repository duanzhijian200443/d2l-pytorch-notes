A strong city walking tour should balance both. Celebrated landmarks provide historical anchors and satisfy visitors’ expectations, but overlooked everyday places reveal how residents actually live: markets, laundromats, corner stores, transit stops, and small parks. Landmarks tell the official story; ordinary spaces add texture, surprise, and social context. Begin with an iconic site, then use nearby streets and routines to complicate its narrative. That approach makes the city feel less like a postcard and more like a living place.
