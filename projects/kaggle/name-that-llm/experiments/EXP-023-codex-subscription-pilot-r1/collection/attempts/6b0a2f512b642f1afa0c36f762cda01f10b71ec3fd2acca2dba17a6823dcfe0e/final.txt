Try a “one page after a daily cue” routine:

- **Pick a reliable cue:** Read one page after brushing your teeth at night or while drinking your morning coffee.
- **Make starting easy:** Keep one enjoyable book beside that spot, with your place marked. Put your phone out of reach.
- **Set a tiny minimum:** One page counts—even during your busiest week. You can keep reading if you want, but extra pages never become tomorrow’s requirement.
- **Skip catch-up:** If you miss a day, simply return at the next cue. There’s no reading debt.
- **Adjust the book:** If you consistently dread opening it, choose something more inviting.

The goal is a routine you can return to, not an ambitious schedule you must maintain perfectly.
