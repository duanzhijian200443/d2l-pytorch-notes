Try a “kind, practical inventory” rather than forcing yourself to finish everything.

Set aside one hour and sort supplies into three groups:

- **Keep:** Projects you genuinely want to return to within the next 6–12 months. Choose based on current interest, not money already spent or who you hoped to become.
- **Pause:** Things you’re unsure about, seasonal hobbies, or projects needing one missing piece. Put these in a labeled box with a review date six months from now.
- **Give away:** Supplies for hobbies that feel heavy, obligatory, or untouched for a long time. Donate to a school, community studio, friend, or local creative group.

For each hobby, ask: *If I had no supplies already, would I choose to start this today?* If not, releasing it may create relief rather than failure.

Keep only a small, visible “next steps” basket for active hobbies. Everything else should be stored out of sight or passed on. Your supplies are not evidence of unfinished obligations; they are resources that can serve you—or someone else—better now.
