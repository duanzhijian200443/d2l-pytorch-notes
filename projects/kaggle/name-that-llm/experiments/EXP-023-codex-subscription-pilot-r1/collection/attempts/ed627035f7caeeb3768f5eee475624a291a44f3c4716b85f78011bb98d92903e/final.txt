Set a “minimum viable reading” goal: **10 minutes or 5 pages daily**, whichever comes first.

Attach it to an existing cue, such as after dinner, during a commute, or before sleep. Keep the current book visible and easy to access—on your pillow, in your bag, or as the first app on your phone.

On busy days, read only one page. The rule is to preserve the habit, not to catch up.

Choose enjoyable, low-friction books rather than “should” books. Track completed days with a simple checkmark, but never restart a streak: missed days are normal.

Once a week, take 20–30 relaxed minutes to read more if you want. Over time, frequent small finishes build confidence and make longer reading sessions feel natural.
