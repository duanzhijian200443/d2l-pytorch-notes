At the airport’s quiet waiting lounge, Mara and Luis found a flat-pack bookshelf abandoned beside a vending machine. Neither lived nearby; Mara was between trains, Luis between countries. With one screwdriver and the instructions upside down, they sorted screws, argued cheerfully, and built the crooked shelf together. By midnight, it stood beneath the departure board, holding travel guides and forgotten newspapers. Their trains arrived minutes apart. They left without exchanging numbers, but each carried the odd comfort of having made a small, useful thing with a stranger.
