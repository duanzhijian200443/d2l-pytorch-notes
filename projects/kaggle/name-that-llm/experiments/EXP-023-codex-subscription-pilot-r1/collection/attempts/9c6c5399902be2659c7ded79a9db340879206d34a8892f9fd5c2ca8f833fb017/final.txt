The gift shop should compare customer demand, profit margins, storage costs, and inventory turnover. Stocking more postcard varieties can attract diverse tastes, encourage browsing, and make the shop feel distinctive, but unpopular designs may remain unsold. Buying deeper quantities of popular designs reduces the risk of stockouts, captures reliable sales, and may lower per-unit costs, though it limits choice and could miss niche demand. Reviewing past sales, customer feedback, seasonal trends, and sales forecasts would help determine the best balance, with periodic adjustments as preferences change.
