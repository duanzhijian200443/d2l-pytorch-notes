Borrowing rarely used household tools is often cheaper and more sustainable than owning them. It avoids purchase costs, storage space, maintenance, repairs, and the risk of a tool becoming obsolete. Borrowing also makes sense for expensive specialty equipment used only once or twice a year. However, it requires access to a reliable lender, flexible timing, and responsibility for returning the tool promptly and in good condition. Availability may be limited when several people need the same item, and borrowed tools may be unfamiliar or poorly maintained. Owning offers immediate access, convenience, and familiarity, especially for urgent repairs or frequently used tools. It also lets you choose the exact quality and features you want. Consider frequency of use, storage, transportation, replacement liability, and the value of your time—not just the purchase price.
