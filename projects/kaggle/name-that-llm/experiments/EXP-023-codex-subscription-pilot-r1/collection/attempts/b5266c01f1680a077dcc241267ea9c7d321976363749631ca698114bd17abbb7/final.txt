**Repair Afternoon – Sunday, 2–5 pm**  
Join us at the Community Hall for a friendly repair afternoon. Bring along one small broken household item and volunteers will take a look with you. They’ll explain the approaches they try and, where possible, help investigate what may be wrong.  

Please note that repairs cannot be guaranteed, but the aim is to learn, share skills, and give useful items another chance. Tea will be available while you wait or chat with others.  

No advance booking is needed—just drop in any time between 2 and 5 pm.
