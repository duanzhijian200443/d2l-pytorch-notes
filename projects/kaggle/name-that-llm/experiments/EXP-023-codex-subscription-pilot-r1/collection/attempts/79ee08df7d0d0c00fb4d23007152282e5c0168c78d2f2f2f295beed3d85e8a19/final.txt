Rain drove Mara into my little shop just before closing. She wandered among the shelves, lingering over a cracked ceramic bird. I assumed she might buy it, but she chose only a postcard. At the door, she paused, then lifted the bird and turned it so its unbroken side faced outside. I thanked her, though I didn’t understand. After she left, I noticed the damaged side resembled a storm cloud—and wondered whether she had been protecting the doorway, the bird, or me.
