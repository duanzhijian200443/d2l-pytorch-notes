**Share Your Memories of the Old Swimming Pool**

Do you remember visiting the old swimming pool—learning to swim, meeting friends, taking part in lessons, or spending summer afternoons there? We are recording local residents’ memories to help preserve the pool’s place in our community’s history.

We are inviting residents to take part in a short interview lasting approximately 20 minutes. You may talk about any memories, stories, or experiences you would like to share. Participation is entirely optional, and you are free to decline or stop the interview at any time.

Before taking part, contributors can choose whether their names are included or whether their contributions remain anonymous. With permission, selected extracts from the interviews will be edited and displayed at the community center for others to read and enjoy.

If you would like to contribute, or would like more information about the project, please contact [name/contact details]. All memories are welcome—whether they are recent or from many years ago.
