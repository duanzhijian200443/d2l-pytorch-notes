The workshop was arranged to make learning practical and accessible. Six benches gave participants dedicated space to measure, cut, and assemble their projects. Tools were hung within everyone’s reach, so learners could find what they needed without searching through drawers or waiting for assistance. Scrap wood was sorted into separate bins, making it easy to choose suitable pieces for practice while keeping reusable material off the floor. This organization helped the group spend more time working and less time looking for supplies or clearing space. With shared tools visible, materials neatly separated, and enough benches for hands-on work, the workshop supported demonstrations, independent practice, and collaboration among participants.
