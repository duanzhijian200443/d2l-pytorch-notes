Requiring reservations for a popular community workshop helps organizers manage capacity, prepare materials, and ensure participants receive enough attention. It also reduces overcrowding and gives attendees confidence that a place will be available. However, reservations can exclude spontaneous visitors or people with limited internet access. No-shows may leave seats unused while others miss out, and managing bookings adds administrative work. A balanced approach could combine reservations with a few walk-in spaces, an accessible booking method, and a waitlist to fill cancellations.
