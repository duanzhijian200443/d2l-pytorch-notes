Run a one- or two-week, low-cost test. Bake a slightly larger batch of the sold-out item while reducing leftover items only modestly. Track when each product sells, how many customers ask for the unavailable item, weather, weekday, and any promotions. You could also offer a simple counter survey or social-media poll asking customers which items they prefer. Compare sales, waste, and profit during the trial. This provides evidence about whether demand is consistent before making broader menu or production changes.
