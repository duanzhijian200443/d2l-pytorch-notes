Build your grocery list around flexible meal options rather than assigning dinners to specific days.

- **Check what you have.** Look through the fridge, freezer, and pantry, especially for leftovers and foods that need using soon.
- **Choose three or four adaptable meals.** Pick dishes with overlapping ingredients: roasted vegetables can become tacos, grain bowls, or an omelet.
- **Buy for fewer dinners than seven.** Leave room for leftovers, eating out, and unexpected plans.
- **Include reliable basics.** Eggs, beans, frozen vegetables, rice, and pasta can fill gaps without spoiling quickly.
- **Separate essentials from extras.** Prioritize breakfast, lunch, snacks, and ingredients needed to finish meals.

Each day, decide what to cook based on your time and what needs using first. Freeze surplus portions promptly.
