Choosing between one famous performer and several lesser-known local acts depends on the festival’s goals.

A famous performer can attract a larger audience, generate publicity, and encourage sponsorship. Fans may travel from outside the area, benefiting nearby shops, restaurants, and hotels. A headline act can also give the festival prestige and make it memorable. However, spending the entire entertainment budget on one person concentrates the risk: cancellation could seriously damage the event. Moreover, the performer’s appeal may be limited to a particular age group or musical taste.

Inviting several local performers offers variety and creates more opportunities to represent the community. A diverse programme can appeal to different audiences, provide entertainment throughout the day, and help emerging artists build their careers. It also strengthens local cultural networks. If one act cancels, the festival can still proceed with relatively little disruption. The main disadvantage is that unfamiliar names may struggle to attract visitors or media attention.

Overall, local performers are probably the stronger choice for a community-focused festival seeking broad participation and lasting local benefits. A famous performer makes more sense if the main objective is to raise the festival’s profile and attract tourists—provided the organisers have a reliable contingency plan.
