The percentages differ because each change is measured from a different starting value. Attendance rose by 10 people from an initial 20, so the percentage increase was \(10/20 \times 100 = 50\%\). It then fell by 10 people from 30, so the percentage decrease was \(10/30 \times 100 \approx 33.3\%\). Although the numerical change was 10 people in both directions, the bases were different: 20 for the increase and 30 for the decrease.
