At dusk, Mrs. Vale entered the little hardware shop as the owner turned the sign to CLOSED. She bought nothing at first, only touched the worn counter where her husband had once chosen paint for their first room. Then she found a single brass hook and placed it gently beside the till.

“For the kitchen,” she said.

The owner wrapped it in brown paper. Outside, rain softened the street. Inside, the shelves waited quietly, almost empty, holding their breath as the bell rang one final time.
