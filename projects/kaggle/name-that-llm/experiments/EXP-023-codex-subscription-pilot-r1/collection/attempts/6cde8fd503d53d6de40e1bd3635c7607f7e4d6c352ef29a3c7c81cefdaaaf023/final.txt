Sort everything into three piles: keep, scan, and recycle. Choose a small archival box or a slim photo album with pocket pages—something that can hold a limited number, so the collection stays modest. Keep only items with strong memories, attractive designs, or personal significance; aim for one or two dozen pieces. Add a simple index card noting dates, places, and who was involved, but skip elaborate captions. For especially fragile or bulky items, take quick phone scans and store them in a clearly named digital folder. Arrange the physical pieces chronologically or by theme, and leave a few empty pockets for future additions. The result is organized, portable, and meaningful without requiring cutting, gluing, or specialized crafting.
