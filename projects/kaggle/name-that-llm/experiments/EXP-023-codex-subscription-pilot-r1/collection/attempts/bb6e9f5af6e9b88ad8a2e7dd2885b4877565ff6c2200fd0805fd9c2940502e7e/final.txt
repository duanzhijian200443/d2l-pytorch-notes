Planning a garden around a strict visual design creates clear lines, coordinated colors, and predictable focal points. It can suit small spaces or formal settings, but often requires regular pruning, weeding, and replacement planting. Allowing plants to spread naturally creates a changing, informal landscape that can support wildlife and reveal unexpected combinations. However, vigorous species may crowd out others or spill onto paths. A balanced approach uses structure for beds and walkways while giving plants freedom within those boundaries.
