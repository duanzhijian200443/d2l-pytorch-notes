Choose recipes by **ingredient overlap**, not just by cuisine or novelty. Start with a “use-first” list of ingredients already in your cupboards, fridge, and freezer, then find several recipes that share their main components—such as chickpeas, tomatoes, herbs, or a particular grain. Plan a small sequence: one recipe using fresh, perishable items first, followed by meals that use the leftovers or sturdier ingredients. Favor flexible dishes like soups, stir-fries, frittatas, curries, and grain bowls, where substitutions are easy. Before shopping, check what you already have, buy only complementary ingredients, and choose recipes that use an entire package. Freeze surplus portions, preserve excess produce, and occasionally schedule a “cupboard challenge” meal to prevent forgotten ingredients from accumulating.
