A map may show the shortest distance between two stops, but that route is not usable if a closed gate blocks it. Drivers must consider real conditions such as closures, restricted access, construction, traffic, road quality, and safety. A slightly longer open route can be faster and more reliable because it avoids delays, detours, or the need to turn around. The best delivery route balances distance with accessibility, travel time, and the likelihood of completing all stops smoothly.
