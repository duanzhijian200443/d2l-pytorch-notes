Use a quick, fair rotation: each person picks a genre or one film suggestion, then draw from the choices randomly. Set a two-minute rule for pitches and a five-minute limit for deciding. Alternate who has the final tie-break each movie night, and keep a shared list so skipped picks carry forward. Occasionally choose a “genre sampler” film that blends interests—comedy-adventure, animated mystery, or action fantasy—so everyone gets something they enjoy.
