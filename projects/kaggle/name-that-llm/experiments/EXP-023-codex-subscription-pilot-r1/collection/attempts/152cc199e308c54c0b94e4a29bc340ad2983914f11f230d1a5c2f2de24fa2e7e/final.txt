Mara slipped into my shop with the rain at her heels, shaking droplets from her coat. I watched her linger over the cracked ceramic bird, turning it carefully in her hands as though deciding whether its damage mattered. In the end, she brought only a postcard to the counter. Before stepping back into the downpour, she returned to the shelf and quietly turned the bird around, leaving its unbroken side facing the door. Somehow, the little thing looked proud again.
