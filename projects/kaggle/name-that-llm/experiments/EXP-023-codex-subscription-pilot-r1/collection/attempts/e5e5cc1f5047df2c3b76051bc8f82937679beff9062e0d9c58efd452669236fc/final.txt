Empty everything onto a table and sort by shape: round, square, rectangular, and unusual sizes. Stack matching containers together, nesting them only when they are clean and dry. Group lids by shape and size beside each stack, then test matches quickly. Recycle or discard cracked, stained, warped, or lidless containers; donate usable extras you realistically will not use.

To prevent buildup, designate one bin or drawer for containers and one upright divider or basket for lids. Keep only a practical set, such as enough for a week of leftovers and lunches. Adopt a “one in, one out” rule for new containers. After washing, immediately pair each lid with its container or return both to their assigned sections, rather than tossing them into a mixed pile.
