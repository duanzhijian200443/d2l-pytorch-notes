Pilot teams are often self-selected enthusiasts who tolerate ambiguity, invest extra effort, and receive close support. Their success may depend on unusual motivation, skills, leadership attention, or simplified conditions. A full rollout includes people with different priorities, workloads, confidence, and resistance to change. At scale, training, support, governance, systems integration, and consistent communication also become harder. The pilot therefore demonstrates potential, not guaranteed organization-wide adoption or sustained performance.
