Adopt a simple, visible change-request process that distinguishes minor clarifications from scope changes. Require every new request to be submitted through one shared form or email template stating: requested change, business reason, deadline, and approver. The project lead should assess impact within one business day and respond with options: include it with no schedule impact, trade it for an existing deliverable, add time/cost through a change order, or defer it to a later phase.

Set a cutoff date for included revisions and communicate it in proposals and kickoff meetings. Keep a change log visible to clients and the internal team. Empower staff to say, “Happy to help—let’s log this so we can confirm impact first.” Review recurring requests monthly to improve scoping and pricing.
