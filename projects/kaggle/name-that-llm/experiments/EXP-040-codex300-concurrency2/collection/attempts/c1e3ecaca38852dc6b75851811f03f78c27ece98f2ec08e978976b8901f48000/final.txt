Today’s display has attracted fewer visitors than it did yesterday. This is an observation, but it does not by itself show that people dislike the new layout. Several factors could explain the difference, including the weather, day of the week, visitor schedules, or the absence of a school group like the one that visited yesterday. Yesterday was warmer, and the school group likely increased attendance, so the two days may not be directly comparable. Before deciding whether to restore the previous layout, we should collect information over several days, compare attendance under similar conditions, observe how visitors use the space, and ask for feedback. If evidence consistently indicates that the new layout is less effective, we can then consider targeted changes rather than immediately reversing everything.
