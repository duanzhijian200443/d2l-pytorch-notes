An unlabeled folder cannot be blue. The statement “Every blue folder is labeled” means that being blue guarantees having a label, so a folder without a label must not be blue. However, you cannot conclude that an unlabeled folder is green, nor can you conclude that it is not green. The statement “Some labeled folders are green” establishes only that at least one folder is both labeled and green; it does not say that all green folders are labeled or that all nonblue folders are green. Thus, an unlabeled folder could be green or another nonblue color without contradicting either premise. Finally, the premises do not establish that any unlabeled folders actually exist; they only constrain such folders if they exist.
