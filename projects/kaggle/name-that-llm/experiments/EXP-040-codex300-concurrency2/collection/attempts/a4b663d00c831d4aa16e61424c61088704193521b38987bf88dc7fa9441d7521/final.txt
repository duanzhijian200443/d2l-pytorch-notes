Today, we observed fewer visitors at the display than yesterday. We also know that the layout recently changed, yesterday’s weather was warmer, and a school group visited yesterday. These are observations, although a single day’s visitor count may not reflect a meaningful trend. The idea that people dislike the new layout is an inference, not a confirmed explanation. Attendance could also have been affected by the cooler weather, the absence of the school group, the day of the week, or other factors. Therefore, moving everything back immediately would be premature. Instead, we should monitor visitor numbers over a longer period, compare similar days, gather visitor feedback, and observe how people interact with the new layout before deciding whether changes are necessary.
