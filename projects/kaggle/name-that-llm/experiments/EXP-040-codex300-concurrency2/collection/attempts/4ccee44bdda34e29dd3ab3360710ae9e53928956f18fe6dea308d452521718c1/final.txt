Your browser saves copies of website files, such as images, styles, and scripts, on your laptop so pages load faster next time. This storage is called a cache. After a website is updated, your browser may still reuse older saved files, making the site look unchanged. Try a hard refresh, which asks the browser to reload files, or clear that site’s cached data. If it still looks old, the website’s servers may also be caching content, so the delay might not be on your laptop.
