Try a gentle three-category review: keep active, pause intentionally, or give away. Gather one hobby at a time and ask yourself whether you genuinely want to do it in the next three months, not whether the person you hoped to become “should” do it. Keep only the supplies for one or two hobbies you feel curious or excited about, and give them a specific, easy next action and a place on your calendar. Pause hobbies that still matter but do not fit your current season; box and label their supplies with a review date six months away, then store them out of sight so they stop creating daily guilt. Give away anything you consistently avoid, have outgrown, or could cheaply replace if interest returns. Consider donating to schools, community groups, friends, or local reuse programs. Set a reasonable space limit, such as one shelf or bin per paused hobby. Remember that releasing supplies is not admitting failure; it is choosing room, attention, and kindness for your present life.
