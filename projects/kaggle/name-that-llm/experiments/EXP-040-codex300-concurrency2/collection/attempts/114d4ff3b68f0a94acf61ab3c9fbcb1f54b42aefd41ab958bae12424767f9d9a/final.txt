Treat each hobby supply as a neutral object, not evidence of failure. Set aside a calm hour and make three categories: keep, pause, and give away. Keep only projects you genuinely expect to enjoy or use within the next six months, with enough time, space, and interest to begin. Pause items that still matter but do not fit this season; pack them neatly in one labeled box with a review date six months from now, so they are out of sight without becoming a permanent burden. Give away supplies for activities that feel like obligation, fantasy-self pressure, or repeated avoidance, especially if someone else could use them now. If you are unsure, choose a small “test kit” and try the hobby once before deciding. Limit your active hobbies to a number that feels manageable, perhaps one creative and one practical pursuit. Thank yourself for the enthusiasm that led you to acquire the materials, then let your current life—not past intentions—guide what remains.
