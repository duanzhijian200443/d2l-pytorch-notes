A pilot led by enthusiastic volunteers benefits from unusually high motivation, patience, expertise, and willingness to solve problems. Participants may tolerate extra work, learn new processes quickly, and provide constructive feedback. A full rollout includes people with varied skills, priorities, incentives, and resistance to change. It also exposes weaknesses hidden by the pilot’s small scale, close support, and selective participation. Without adequate training, resources, leadership, and workflow integration, the project’s early success may therefore prove difficult to reproduce across the wider organization.
