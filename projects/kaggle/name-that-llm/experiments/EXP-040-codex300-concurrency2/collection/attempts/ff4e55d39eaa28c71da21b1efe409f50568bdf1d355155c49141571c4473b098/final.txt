Every Thursday, Mara repaired the clocks in the old railway station, returning each one to perfect time. Only the brass clock above Platform Three remained seven minutes slow. When the stationmaster asked why, Mara admitted her father had once worked there. He had missed his train by seven minutes, met her mother while waiting, and built their life together. After he died, Mara could not erase that accident from the station’s memory. So she kept the clock slow, honoring the delay that had given her a family.
