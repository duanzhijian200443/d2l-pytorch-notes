Mara stood between the tomato beds, holding a weathered wooden stake whose label had vanished in the rain. She had planted basil there—or perhaps peppers—but the unfamiliar purple shoots offered no clues. “Those are eggplants,” said a voice from across the path. An older man named Luis approached, smiling, with a roll of masking tape in his pocket. He admitted he had moved the stake by mistake while repairing the irrigation hose. Mara laughed, relieved that neither of them had ruined anything. Together they made new labels, then spent the afternoon comparing recipes and pulling weeds. Before leaving, Luis invited her to share his family’s eggplant stew at Saturday’s potluck. Mara arrived expecting only to identify a plant and went home with a new friend.
