Although both cultures experience the same sunrise, daylight, sunset, and night, they may organize time according to different needs and beliefs. Farming, trade, religious rituals, work patterns, climate, and technology can make particular moments more important in one society than another. One culture may emphasize dawn and dusk for prayer, while another focuses on market hours or shifts of labor. Calendars, language, and inherited traditions then reinforce these divisions, turning the shared natural cycle into distinct meaningful periods.
