The neighborhood reading room should trial later opening hours on two evenings each week for one month, rather than attempting to open every evening with limited volunteer support. A designated quiet corner would give readers a reliable space for focused study, while a noticeboard could improve communication about events, schedules, and volunteer opportunities. At the end of the trial, feedback from visitors and volunteers, along with attendance figures, should be reviewed to decide whether the arrangement is useful, manageable, and worth continuing.
