A pilot often succeeds because volunteers are motivated, adaptable, and willing to invest extra time in solving problems. They may also have stronger skills, supportive managers, and a positive attitude toward change. A wider rollout includes people with different workloads, confidence levels, priorities, and access to training or resources. Processes that work informally for a small group may become unclear or inefficient at scale. Success therefore requires realistic support, communication, standardization, and attention to resistance and practical constraints.
