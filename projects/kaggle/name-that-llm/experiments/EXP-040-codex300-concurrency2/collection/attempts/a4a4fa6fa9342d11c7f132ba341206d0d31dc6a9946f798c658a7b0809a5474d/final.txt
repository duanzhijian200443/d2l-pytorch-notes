Choose a daily reading minimum so small that a busy week cannot easily defeat it: read for ten minutes or five pages, whichever comes first. Attach the routine to something you already do, such as drinking morning coffee, taking lunch, or getting into bed. Keep the current book visible and your phone out of reach. On unusually hectic days, allow a two-page “maintenance” version rather than skipping entirely. Stop when the minimum is complete, even if you could continue; extra reading is optional, not required. Mark each session on a calendar, but do not try to compensate for missed days. At the end of the week, briefly note whether the book still interests you. If it does not, switch books without guilt. Consistency matters more than an impressive schedule.
