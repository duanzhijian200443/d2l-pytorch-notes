Empty the cupboard and gather every container and lid, including those in the dishwasher, refrigerator, and lunch bags. Sort containers by shape and size, then group lids the same way to make matching faster. Test each likely pair rather than assuming it fits. Recycle unmatched pieces where accepted, and discard anything cracked, warped, or unsuitable for food storage. Keep only enough matched sets for your household’s normal leftovers and packed meals. Nest containers neatly and store lids upright in a small bin, or attach lids if space allows. Set a firm storage limit and follow a one-in, one-out rule. Buy replacements from one compatible range, avoid saving unnecessary takeaway tubs, and check for stray pieces during a quick monthly cupboard tidy.
