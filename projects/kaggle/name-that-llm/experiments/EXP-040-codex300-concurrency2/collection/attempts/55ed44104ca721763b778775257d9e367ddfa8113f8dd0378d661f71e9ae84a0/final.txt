Today, the display has fewer visitors than it did previously, and yesterday the weather was warmer and a school group visited. These are observations. It is possible that the lower attendance is related to the cooler weather, the absence of a school group, the new layout, or other factors, but we do not yet know the cause. Saying that people dislike the new layout is an inference, not an established fact. Likewise, deciding to restore the previous arrangement immediately would be a premature conclusion. We should compare attendance across several days, consider visitor feedback, and account for weather, group visits, promotions, and other relevant conditions. If the evidence consistently suggests that the layout is discouraging visitors, we can then make a measured adjustment.
