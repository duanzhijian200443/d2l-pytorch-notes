An item costing 100 units is first discounted by 10 percent, reducing its price by 10 units to 90 units. The shop then adds 10 percent to this reduced price, but 10 percent of 90 is only 9 units. Adding 9 units gives a final price of 99 units. The percentages are applied to different base amounts: the discount uses 100, while the increase uses 90. Therefore, the final price is 1 unit below the original price.
