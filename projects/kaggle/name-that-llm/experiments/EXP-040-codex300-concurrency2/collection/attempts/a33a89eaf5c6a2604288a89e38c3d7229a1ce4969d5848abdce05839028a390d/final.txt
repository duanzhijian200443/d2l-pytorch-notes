Mara returned the borrowed ladder just before dusk, carrying it across the wet alley to Mr. Vale’s narrow blue house. He thanked her, then noticed the silver paint on her sleeve. She admitted she had used the ladder to finish a mural her sister had begun before leaving town. Curious, he followed Mara to the courtyard, where painted swallows swept across a brick wall toward a golden horizon. Mr. Vale stood silently for a moment, blinking behind his thick glasses. “Your sister painted one of those birds on my shop years ago,” he said. From his pocket, he produced a faded photograph proving it. Then he smiled and mentioned a neighborhood supper beneath the mural that evening. Mara hesitated, looked at the gathering lights, and accepted his invitation.
