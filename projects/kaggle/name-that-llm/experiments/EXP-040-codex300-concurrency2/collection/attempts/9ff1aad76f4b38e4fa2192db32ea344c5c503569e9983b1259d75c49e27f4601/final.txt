Although both cultures experience the same natural alternation of daylight and darkness, they may interpret and divide it according to different social needs, beliefs, and environments. Agricultural routines, religious practices, work schedules, meal customs, climate, and technologies such as clocks can make particular moments more significant. One culture might define periods by sunrise, prayer, or heat, while another emphasizes fixed hours, commuting, or leisure. Thus, the physical cycle is shared, but its meaningful boundaries are culturally constructed.
