Recommend a one-month trial in which the reading room stays open late on two evenings each week, rather than attempting daily extended hours with limited volunteers. Set aside a clearly marked quiet corner to support focused reading, while keeping the main space welcoming for conversation. A simple noticeboard could share schedules, volunteer opportunities, local events, and feedback. At the end of the trial, review attendance, volunteer workload, and comments from users before deciding whether to continue or adjust the arrangement.
