Requiring reservations for a popular community workshop helps organizers control attendance, prepare enough materials, ensure safe capacity, and give participants a more focused experience. It can also reduce overcrowding and provide useful information about demand. However, reservations may disadvantage people without reliable internet access, flexible schedules, or advance notice. No-shows can leave spaces unused, while waitlists and cancellation rules create extra administrative work. A simple booking system, reminders, and a few walk-in spaces can balance predictability with accessibility.
