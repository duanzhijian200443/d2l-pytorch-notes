Empty a large workspace and group all containers by shape and size, placing lids in separate matching piles. Match each container immediately, testing the seal; recycle cracked, warped, stained, or duplicate pieces, and keep only a practical number of complete sets for your household. Store matched containers nested by size, with their lids vertically in a bin or clipped to each container, so pairs remain visible and easy to grab. Create a “one in, one out” rule: whenever a new container enters the kitchen, donate or recycle an older complete set. Avoid keeping takeout tubs unless they serve a specific purpose, and schedule a quick check every few months to remove lonely lids and containers before clutter rebuilds.
