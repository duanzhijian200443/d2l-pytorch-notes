A metal spoon can feel colder than a wooden spoon even though both are actually at the same room temperature. Metal conducts heat very well, so when you touch it, heat quickly flows from your warm hand into the spoon. Your hand cools down faster, making the spoon feel cold. Wood is a poor conductor, so it takes heat from your hand much more slowly. The wood isn’t warmer; it simply doesn’t remove heat from your skin as quickly as metal does.
