To make the recipe for six people instead of four, scale the broth by the same factor as the number of servings. Divide the new serving count by the original serving count: six divided by four equals 1.5. This means you need one and a half times as much of every ingredient. Since the original recipe uses three cups of broth, multiply three by 1.5 to get 4.5 cups. Therefore, you need four and a half cups of broth for six people. You can also check this by finding the amount per person: three cups divided among four people is three quarters of a cup each. Multiplying that amount by six gives the same total of four and a half cups.
