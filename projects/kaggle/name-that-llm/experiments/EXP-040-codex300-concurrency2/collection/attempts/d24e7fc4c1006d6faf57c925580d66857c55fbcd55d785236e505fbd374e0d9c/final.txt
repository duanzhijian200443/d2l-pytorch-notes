For an unlabeled folder, one conclusion is justified: it cannot be blue. Since every blue folder is labeled, any blue folder must have a label; therefore, a folder without a label is not blue. However, no justified conclusion can be drawn about its color beyond that. The statement that some labeled folders are green does not mean that all green folders are labeled, nor does it say anything about every unlabeled folder. Thus, the unlabeled folder could be green, or it could be another color. We also cannot conclude that it is the only unlabeled folder, that all other folders are labeled, or that the existence of green labeled folders has any particular relationship to it. The premises provide no further information about unlabeled folders.
