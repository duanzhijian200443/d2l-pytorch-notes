Create a consistent closing ritual: save your work, write down tomorrow’s first task, and shut the laptop with intention. Clear the table completely, returning work items to a drawer or basket. Change the lighting, play a brief “end of day” song, or light a candle associated only with relaxation. Wash your hands or make a fresh drink before preparing dinner. Finally, take a short walk, stretch, or pause for three deep breaths to signal that work is over and personal time has begun.
