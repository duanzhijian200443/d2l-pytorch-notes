Every Thursday, Mr. Vale brought his kitchen clock to Lena’s shop, complaining that it lost three minutes. She cleaned the gears twice before his daughter explained: he set it beside his wife’s photograph and waited while Lena worked. Since the funeral, it was the only place he regularly went. Lena could have replaced the worn spring. Instead, she adjusted it just enough to keep losing time. When he returned the following Thursday, she put the kettle on before opening the clock.
