Set a deliberately small reading routine that can survive your busiest days: read for ten minutes or five pages, whichever comes first, at the same dependable time, such as while drinking coffee, commuting, or before bed. Keep one book visible and choose something genuinely appealing rather than something you feel obligated to finish. On chaotic days, read only one page; maintaining the habit matters more than meeting a quota. At the end of each session, stop while you still feel interested and leave the book ready for tomorrow. Once a week, review your progress without judgment and adjust the book or schedule if needed. The goal is not to complete an impressive number of books, but to make reading an easy, repeatable part of ordinary life.
