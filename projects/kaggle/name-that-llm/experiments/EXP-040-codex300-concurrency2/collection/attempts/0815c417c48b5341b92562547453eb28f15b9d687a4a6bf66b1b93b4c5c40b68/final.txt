Each morning, Mara announced the same local departures at Westbridge Station. One rainy Tuesday, an old timetable slipped from a drawer: 6:17 to Larkhill, platform four. No clerk remembered Larkhill, and platform four ended at a brick wall. Still, Mara announced it softly. At 6:17, nothing arrived. After her shift, she checked the wall and found a rusted brass plaque near the floor: “Larkhill Line—closed 1962,” beside a child’s lost blue button.
