To scale the recipe from four people to six people, compare the new number of servings with the original number: six divided by four equals 1.5. This means every ingredient must be multiplied by 1.5 to keep the recipe’s proportions and flavor the same. The original recipe uses three cups of broth, so multiply three cups by 1.5. The calculation is 3 × 1.5 = 4.5 cups. Therefore, the recipe needs four and a half cups of broth for six people. Another way to see this is that three cups for four people equals three-quarters of a cup per person. Multiplying three-quarters of a cup by six people also gives four and a half cups of broth.
