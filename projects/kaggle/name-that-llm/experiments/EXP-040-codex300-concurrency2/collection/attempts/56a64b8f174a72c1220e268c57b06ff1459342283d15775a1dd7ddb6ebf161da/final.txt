Choose a “minimum reading” routine that is too small to fail: read five pages or for ten minutes each day, whichever comes first. Attach it to an existing cue, such as after breakfast, during a commute, or before sleep, and keep the current book visible and easy to reach. On busy days, count even one page as success; the goal is to preserve the habit, not compensate for missed time. Pick enjoyable, accessible books rather than worthy-but-demanding ones, and stop at a natural pause so returning feels easy. Reserve one slightly longer session, perhaps 30 minutes on the weekend, only if it feels welcome. Track completed days with a simple checkmark, then reassess after a month.
