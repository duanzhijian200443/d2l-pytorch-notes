At day’s end, close the laptop, put it in a drawer or bag, and clear work items from the table. Take two minutes to write tomorrow’s first task, then switch on a different lamp, play a favorite song, or light a candle. Wash your hands, change clothes, and step outside briefly if possible. Set the table with a placemat or coaster that signals “home mode,” then make tea, share a snack, or sit quietly for a few breaths before relaxing.
