Every morning, Mara announced the trains at Brookfield Station, where the same commuters waited beneath the same leaking roof. One Tuesday, she found an old timetable tucked behind the microphone. It listed the 8:17 to Bellmere, a train nobody remembered, though the platform number was correct. She asked passengers, clerks, even the retired stationmaster. No one had heard of Bellmere. At 8:17, nothing arrived. After work, Mara noticed a fresh brass key on the timetable, labeled “Locker 8:17.”
