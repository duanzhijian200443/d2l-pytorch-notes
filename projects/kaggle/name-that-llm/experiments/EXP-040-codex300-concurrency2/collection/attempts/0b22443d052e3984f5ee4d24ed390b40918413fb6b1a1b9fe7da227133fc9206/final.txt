A pilot project can succeed because enthusiastic volunteers are unusually motivated, adaptable, and willing to tolerate problems. They may also receive extra training, resources, and attention that are difficult to provide at scale. When the project reaches everyone, participants vary more in skills, needs, workloads, and willingness to change. Practical barriers and resistance become more visible, while support may be stretched thin. Therefore, pilot success demonstrates potential, but does not guarantee that the approach will work equally well across the whole organization.
