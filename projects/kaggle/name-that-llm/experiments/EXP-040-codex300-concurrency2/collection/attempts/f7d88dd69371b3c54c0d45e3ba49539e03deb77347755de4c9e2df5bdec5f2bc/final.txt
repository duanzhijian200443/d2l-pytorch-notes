Try reading for five minutes after something you already do each day, such as brushing your teeth or making evening tea. Keep one appealing book beside that spot so starting takes almost no effort. On especially busy days, read just one paragraph; that counts as keeping the routine, not failing it. You can continue when you feel interested, but avoid increasing your daily minimum after a good week. If you miss a day, simply return at the next opportunity without catching up. Choose books you genuinely want to read, and give yourself permission to abandon ones you dislike. The aim is to make reading an easy, pleasant part of ordinary life rather than another demanding schedule to maintain.
