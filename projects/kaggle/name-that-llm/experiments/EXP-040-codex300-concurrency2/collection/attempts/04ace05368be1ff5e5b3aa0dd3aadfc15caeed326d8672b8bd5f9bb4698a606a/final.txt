“Why is that little square bare?” the visitor asked. “Did nothing grow there?” The gardener smiled. “Something did, once.” “Was it a rare flower?” “No. My daughter’s toy spade marked the spot where she first helped me plant.” “So you leave it empty for her?” “For the child she was, and for the woman who still visits each spring.” “Does she remember?” “She asks why that patch is bare. Then she kneels beside it, just as she did before.”
