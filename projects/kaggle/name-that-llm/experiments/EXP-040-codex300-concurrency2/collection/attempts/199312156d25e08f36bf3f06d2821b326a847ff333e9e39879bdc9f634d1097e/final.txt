The display has fewer visitors today than it did yesterday. That is an observation; the claim that people dislike the new layout is an inference we have not yet verified. We also know that yesterday’s weather was warmer and that a school group visited, both of which could help explain the difference in attendance. The new layout might be affecting visitor interest, but the available information does not establish that it caused the decline. Rather than move everything back immediately, we should compare attendance across several days with similar weather and account for group visits. Asking visitors for feedback and observing how they interact with the display would help us decide whether changes to the layout are warranted.
