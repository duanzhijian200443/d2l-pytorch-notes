Making a town’s central square visually uniform can create harmony, strengthen its historic character, and make public space feel orderly. Consistent signs may also prevent aggressive advertising from overwhelming architecture, helping visitors appreciate the square as a whole. However, allowing every shop a distinctive sign gives businesses a clear identity and lets owners express creativity, local traditions, and personality. This variety can make the square lively and memorable while helping customers distinguish shops easily. Too much uniformity risks producing a bland, overly controlled environment, whereas unrestricted signs can cause visual clutter. A balanced approach could therefore set limits on size, lighting, and placement while permitting different colours and designs, preserving the square’s shared character without sacrificing individual expression.
