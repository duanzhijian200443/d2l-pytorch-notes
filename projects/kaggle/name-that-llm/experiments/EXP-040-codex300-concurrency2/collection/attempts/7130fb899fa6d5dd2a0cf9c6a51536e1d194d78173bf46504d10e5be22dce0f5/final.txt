During her tea break, station announcer Ruth found an old timetable behind the office radiator. Between the 8:12 and 8:26 was an 8:19 to Bell Street. Nobody remembered it, not even Colin, who had worked there forty years. Ruth checked the platform every morning that week. Nothing arrived at 8:19 except commuters carrying coffee. On Friday, she unfolded the timetable completely. Tucked inside was a child’s pencil drawing of a train. Its destination board said Bell Street. Underneath, in Colin’s handwriting, was “Our house.”
