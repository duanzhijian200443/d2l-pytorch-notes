Your browser saves copies of website files—such as images, styles, and scripts—so pages load faster next time. When the site is updated, your laptop may still display these older cached files, making it look unchanged. Try refreshing while holding Shift or Ctrl (Command on a Mac), or clear the browser’s cache. You can also open the site in a private window or wait for the cache to expire. If the problem persists, the website’s server or an internet provider may also be caching the old version.
