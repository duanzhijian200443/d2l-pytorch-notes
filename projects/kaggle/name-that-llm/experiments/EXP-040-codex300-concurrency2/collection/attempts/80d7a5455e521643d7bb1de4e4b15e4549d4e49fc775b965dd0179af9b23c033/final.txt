Try a five-minute closing ritual: jot down tomorrow’s first task, close your tabs, and silence work notifications. Put your laptop, charger, and notes in a basket out of sight, then wipe the table. Swap your work mug for a water glass, add a placemat, or change the lighting to signal a different use of the space. A short walk, a favorite song, or changing into comfortable clothes can provide a transition before you return to eat or relax.
