Requiring reservations for a popular community workshop can make attendance more predictable, prevent overcrowding, improve safety, and help organizers prepare sufficient materials, equipment, and instructors. It may also give participants confidence that they have a guaranteed place. However, reservations can exclude people who lack reliable internet access, have unpredictable schedules, or miss registration windows. No-shows may leave unused spaces, while administrative work increases. A balanced system could combine reservations with a waitlist and several walk-in spots to preserve accessibility and flexibility.
