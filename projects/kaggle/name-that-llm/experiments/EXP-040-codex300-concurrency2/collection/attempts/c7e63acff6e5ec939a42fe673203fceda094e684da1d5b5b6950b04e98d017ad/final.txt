The reading room should trial opening late twice a week for one month, offering more flexible access without overstretching its limited volunteer team. A designated quiet corner would support readers who need a calm space, while a simple noticeboard could share events, updates, and volunteering opportunities. Opening every evening is not currently realistic, so the trial should focus on two reliably staffed nights. After a month, feedback from visitors and volunteers, alongside attendance figures, can guide whether to continue or adjust the arrangement.
