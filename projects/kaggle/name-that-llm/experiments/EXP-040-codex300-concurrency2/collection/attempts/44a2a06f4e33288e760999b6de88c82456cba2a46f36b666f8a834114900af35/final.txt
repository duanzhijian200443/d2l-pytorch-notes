The attendance increased from 20 to 30 people, a gain of 10. Because the original number was 20, the percentage increase was 10 ÷ 20 = 50%. When attendance later fell from 30 back to 20, the loss was still 10 people, but this time the starting number was 30. Therefore, the percentage decrease was 10 ÷ 30, or about 33.3%. The percentages differ because they are calculated using different starting amounts, even though the numerical change is the same.
