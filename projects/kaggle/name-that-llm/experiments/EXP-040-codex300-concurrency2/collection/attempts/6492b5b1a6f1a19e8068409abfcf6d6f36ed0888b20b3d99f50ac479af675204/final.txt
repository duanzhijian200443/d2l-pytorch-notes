The increase is measured from the original attendance of twenty people. Gaining ten people therefore represents 10 divided by 20, or a 50 percent increase. The decrease, however, is measured from the new attendance of thirty people. Losing the same ten people represents 10 divided by 30, or about a 33.3 percent decrease. The percentages differ because percentage change depends on the starting value, and the starting values for the rise and fall are not the same.
