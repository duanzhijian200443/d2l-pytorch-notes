Communicate the delay promptly, clearly, and honestly, without speculating about a completion date that cannot yet be supported. Explain what has changed, the current impact, and the main factors preventing a reliable revised estimate. Take responsibility for the communication, even if the cause lies elsewhere, and acknowledge the effect on customers, colleagues, or stakeholders. Describe the actions underway to resolve the issue, such as investigating the root cause, adding resources, reprioritizing work, or consulting specialists. Instead of offering false certainty, provide the next concrete checkpoint: for example, commit to sharing an updated forecast by a specific date after the outstanding information is reviewed. Reassure stakeholders that progress will be reported regularly and identify any decisions, support, or mitigation they can provide now. Use a calm, factual tone and avoid minimizing the problem or assigning blame. A message such as, “We are behind schedule, and I do not want to give you an unreliable date. We are assessing the remaining work and will provide a credible forecast by Friday, with updates twice weekly,” demonstrates accountability, transparency, and respect.
