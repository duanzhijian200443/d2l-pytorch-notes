During the quiet hour after lunch, station announcer Mara found an old timetable behind the ticket printer. Between the 2:10 local and the 2:45 express, it listed a 2:27 train to Bellweather. The clerk frowned; the drivers laughed; even the computer showed nothing. Still, at 2:26, Mara announced it. No train arrived, only a warm gust along the empty platform. Later, sweeping near the edge, she found a fresh blue ticket stamped Bellweather, 2:27, that day.
