Providing identical materials promotes fairness because everyone begins with the same resources, reducing the influence of income, access, or preparation. It also makes the workshop easier to organize: instructors can plan specific activities, explain tools once, and solve common problems efficiently. However, uniform materials may limit experimentation and make participants’ work feel similar. Allowing people to bring whatever they have can encourage creativity, resourcefulness, and personal expression, since unusual objects may inspire unexpected ideas. It may also be convenient for organizers because fewer supplies need to be purchased. Yet this approach can disadvantage participants who own fewer or lower-quality materials, and instructors may struggle to support many different tools. A balanced solution is to provide a basic shared kit while inviting optional personal materials, combining equal access with creative freedom.
