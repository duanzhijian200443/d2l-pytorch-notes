Communicate the delay promptly, clearly, and without speculation. State what has changed, the impact on the original plan, and the facts currently known: “We have identified issues that mean we will not meet the revised completion date.” Briefly explain the cause in terms stakeholders can understand, while avoiding blame or overly technical detail. Acknowledge the effect on their priorities and take responsibility for managing it. Be explicit that a reliable new date is not yet available, rather than offering an optimistic estimate that may need to be withdrawn. Explain what work is underway to assess the remaining effort, resolve blockers, and reduce risk. Provide a specific date or cadence for the next update, even if that update may only refine the forecast: “We will share a confirmed timeline, or our best-supported range, by Friday.” Describe any decisions, support, or trade-offs needed from stakeholders. Maintain a calm, accountable tone, invite questions, and continue regular updates so uncertainty does not become silence.
