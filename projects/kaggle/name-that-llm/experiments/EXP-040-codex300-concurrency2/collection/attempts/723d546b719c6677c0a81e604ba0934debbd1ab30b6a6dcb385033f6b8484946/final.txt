Requiring reservations for a popular community workshop helps organizers manage space, prepare enough materials, and maintain safe instructor-to-participant ratios. Attendees also gain confidence that a place will be available, reducing wasted trips and long waits. However, reservations can discourage spontaneous participation and disadvantage people with limited internet access or unpredictable schedules. No-shows may leave valuable spaces unused while others are turned away. Offering phone bookings, a waiting list, and a few walk-in places can balance efficiency with accessibility and community inclusion.
