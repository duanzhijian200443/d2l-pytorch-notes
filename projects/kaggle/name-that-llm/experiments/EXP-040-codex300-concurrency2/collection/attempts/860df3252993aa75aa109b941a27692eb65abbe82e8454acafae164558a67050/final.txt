Making a town’s central square visually uniform can create a strong sense of order, harmony, and shared identity. Consistent signs, colors, and materials may make the area feel welcoming, easier to navigate, and connected to local history or civic values. This approach can also reduce visual clutter and support a polished public image that attracts visitors. Allowing every shop a distinctive sign, however, celebrates individuality and encourages creativity. Unique designs help businesses express their character, make the square more lively, and give visitors memorable landmarks. They can also foster healthy competition and reflect the diversity of the community. The best approach may combine both: establish basic guidelines for scale, placement, and quality while giving each shop freedom to develop an original and recognizable identity.
