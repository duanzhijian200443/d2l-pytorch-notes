Empty the cupboard and group containers by shape, size, and brand. Match each lid to its container, testing that it fits securely and checking for cracks, stains, warping, or unpleasant odors. Recycle or discard damaged pieces, and set aside orphaned containers and lids in a temporary box. After a month, donate, recycle, or discard anything still unmatched; keep only useful, stackable sets that fit your storage space. Store containers nested by size and lids upright in a divider or small bin, so every piece remains visible. To prevent future buildup, keep a strict limit on how many you own, avoid accepting disposable containers, and immediately discard replacements when one becomes unusable. Before buying another, check whether an existing container can serve the same purpose.
