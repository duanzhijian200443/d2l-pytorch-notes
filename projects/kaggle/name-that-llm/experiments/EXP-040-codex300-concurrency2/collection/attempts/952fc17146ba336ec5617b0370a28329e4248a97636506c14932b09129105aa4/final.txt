At day’s end, close every work tab, shut down the computer, and place devices and papers in a box or basket out of sight. Wipe the table, then change the lighting by switching off a task lamp and turning on a warmer one. Take a short walk, change clothes, or wash your hands and face before returning. Setting the table with a placemat, candle, or favorite drink can further signal that the space has shifted from office back to home.
