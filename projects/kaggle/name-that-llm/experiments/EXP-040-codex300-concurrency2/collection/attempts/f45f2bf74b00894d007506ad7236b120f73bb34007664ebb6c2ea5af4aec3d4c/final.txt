Requiring reservations helps a popular community workshop control crowding, ensure enough tools and staff, improve safety, and give participants a clearer, more equitable chance to attend. It also lets organizers plan materials and reduce long waits. However, reservations can exclude people with limited internet access, unpredictable schedules, or language barriers. No-shows may leave unused spaces, while strict booking rules can make the workshop feel less spontaneous and welcoming. A waitlist and walk-in slots can balance these concerns.
