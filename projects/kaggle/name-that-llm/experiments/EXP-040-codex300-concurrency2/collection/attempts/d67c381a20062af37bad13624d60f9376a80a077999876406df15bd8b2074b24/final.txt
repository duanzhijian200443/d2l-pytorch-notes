“Why is that little square bare?” the visitor asked. “Did you run out of seeds?” “No.” “Is the soil poor?” “It’s the richest patch here.” “Then why not plant tomatoes, or lavender, or anything at all?” The gardener knelt and brushed a fallen leaf from the dark earth. “My wife loved this garden,” he said. “But she always wanted somewhere to stand without crushing a flower.” The visitor looked at the empty square. “So you kept her place?” “I keep it open,” he replied.
