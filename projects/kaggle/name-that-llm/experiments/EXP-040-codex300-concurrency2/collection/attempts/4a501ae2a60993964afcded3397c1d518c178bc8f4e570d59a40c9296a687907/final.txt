To adjust the recipe from four people to six, multiply every ingredient by the same scaling factor. The factor is found by dividing the new number of servings by the original number: 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5. Therefore, the broth should also be multiplied by 1.5. Starting with three cups, calculate 3 × 1.5 = 4.5 cups. Thus, you need 4.5 cups of broth, which is equivalent to 4 cups plus 1/2 cup. This amount preserves the recipe’s original proportions while providing enough broth for six people instead of four. If measuring precisely is inconvenient, you can use four and a half cups directly, ensuring that the other ingredients are increased by the same factor of one and a half.
