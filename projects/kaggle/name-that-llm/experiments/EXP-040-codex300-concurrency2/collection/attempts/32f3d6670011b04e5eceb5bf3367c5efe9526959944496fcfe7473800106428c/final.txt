Your browser saves copies of website files, such as images, styles, and scripts, so pages load faster on later visits. After a site is updated, your laptop may still display those stored files instead of downloading the newest versions. Refreshing the page may help, but a hard refresh usually forces the browser to fetch fresh content. You can also clear the browser’s cache or try a private window. Sometimes a company network, service worker, or content delivery network may also temporarily serve an older version.
