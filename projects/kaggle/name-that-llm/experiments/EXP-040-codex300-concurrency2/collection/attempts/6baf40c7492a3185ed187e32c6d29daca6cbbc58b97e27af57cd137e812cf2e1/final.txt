Providing identical materials promotes fairness because every participant begins with the same resources and no one is disadvantaged by cost, access, or preparation. It also makes instructions simpler and allows the workshop leader to plan activities reliably. However, uniform supplies may limit experimentation and can make projects look similar. Letting people bring their own materials encourages creativity, since participants can use familiar tools, unusual objects, and personal styles. It may also be more convenient for organizers, who need not purchase or distribute everything. Yet this approach can create inequalities when some people arrive with expensive or plentiful supplies while others have very little. A balanced solution is to provide a basic shared kit for everyone while inviting optional personal materials.
