Place a slim, washable shoe mat along one wall, leaving a clear walking path to the door. Assign each roommate a section of the mat, with everyday shoes lined up heel-to-wall and pairs kept together. Store less-used shoes inside existing closets, under beds, or in bedroom corners, and keep only one or two frequently worn pairs in the entryway. Agree that no shoes may sit directly in front of the door, and make a quick nightly reset part of everyone’s routine.
