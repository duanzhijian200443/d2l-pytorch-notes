The first room begins with twelve chairs. When four chairs are moved to the second room, subtract four from the original twelve: 12 − 4 = 8. At this intermediate point, there are eight chairs remaining in the first room, while four chairs are in the second room. Next, three chairs are brought back from the second room to the first room. Add those three chairs to the eight already there: 8 + 3 = 11. Therefore, after the four chairs are moved out and then three are returned, eleven chairs remain in the first room. Only one chair is still in the second room, because four were moved there and three came back.
