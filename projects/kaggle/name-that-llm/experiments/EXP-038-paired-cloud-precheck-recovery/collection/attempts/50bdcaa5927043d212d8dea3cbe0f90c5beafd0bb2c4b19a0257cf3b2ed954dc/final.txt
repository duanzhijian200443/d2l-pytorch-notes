Mark a clear “door swing” zone on the floor with painter’s tape and keep it completely empty. Assign each roommate one narrow wall section or corner for a single pair of everyday shoes, with all other pairs stored in their bedrooms. Use an existing boot tray, shallow cardboard box, or washable mat to define each person’s spot and contain dirt. Make a simple rule: shoes must be aligned toe-in against the wall, and guests’ shoes go in one temporary shared corner.
