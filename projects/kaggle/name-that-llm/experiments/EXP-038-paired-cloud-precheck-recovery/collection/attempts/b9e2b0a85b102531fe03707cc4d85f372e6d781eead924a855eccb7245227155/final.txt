Create a shared “landing strip” along one wall using a long washable mat, shallow trays, or even flattened cardboard lined with a towel to define shoe spaces without adding furniture. Assign each roommate an equal section and set a limit of two everyday pairs in the entryway; seasonal, wet, or extra shoes stay in bedrooms or closets. Keep a clear door-swing zone marked with tape, and agree that any shoes outside the marked area get moved to their owner’s room each evening.
