Use the existing wall and floor space strategically: place a slim, washable shoe mat just inside the entrance and limit it to one pair per person. Store additional shoes in sturdy, stackable bins or reusable fabric cubes along the least obstructive wall, keeping them below knee height. Assign each roommate a labeled container and agree that shoes must be put away immediately after entering. Leave a clear walkway at least as wide as the door, and rotate seasonal footwear to reduce clutter.
