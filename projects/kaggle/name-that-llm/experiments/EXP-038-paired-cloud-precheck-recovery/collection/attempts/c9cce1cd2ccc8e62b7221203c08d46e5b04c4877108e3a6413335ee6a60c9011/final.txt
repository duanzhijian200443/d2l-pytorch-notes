The first room starts with twelve chairs. When four chairs are moved from the first room to the second room, subtract four from the original twelve: 12 − 4 = 8. At this intermediate point, eight chairs remain in the first room, while four are in the second room. Then three chairs are brought back from the second room to the first room. Add those three returned chairs to the eight already there: 8 + 3 = 11. Therefore, after both movements are complete, eleven chairs remain in the first room. The key is to account for the chairs in two stages: first remove the four chairs, and then restore the three that return, rather than subtracting all four permanently.
