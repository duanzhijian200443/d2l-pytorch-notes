The room starts with twelve chairs. When four chairs are moved to the second room, subtract four from the original twelve: 12 − 4 = 8 chairs remain in the first room. This is the intermediate step, showing the situation immediately after the first move and before any chairs return. Next, three of those chairs are brought back from the second room to the first room, so add three to the eight already there: 8 + 3 = 11. Therefore, after both movements are complete, eleven chairs remain in the first room. The second room temporarily receives four chairs, but because three are returned, only one chair ultimately stays there, while the first room ends with eleven chairs.
