Designate one wall as the shoe zone and line up a washable mat or shallow tray there to define each person’s space. Give each roommate a labeled section sized for one daily pair, with all other shoes kept in bedrooms. Place a small basket or tote by the door for slippers, umbrellas, or temporary overflow, and agree that anything outside the marked zone gets moved into its owner’s room each evening. Rotate responsibility for straightening the area weekly.
