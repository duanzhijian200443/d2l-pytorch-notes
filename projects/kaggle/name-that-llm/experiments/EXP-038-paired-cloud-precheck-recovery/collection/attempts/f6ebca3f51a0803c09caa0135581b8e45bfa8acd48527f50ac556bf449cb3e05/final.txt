Run a six-week pilot in one high-traffic fiction section: add a small, clearly labeled “Try Someone New” display at the end of an existing shelf, featuring 12 books by lesser-known authors organized within familiar genres rather than in a separate, hard-to-find area. Each featured book should have a short staff-written card naming a comparable well-known author, a one-sentence reason to try it, and a QR code linking to a brief sample or recommendation page. Refresh half the titles every two weeks, while keeping the display’s location and signage consistent. Compare results with a similar fiction section in another store or with sales from the same six-week period last year, tracking unit sales of featured titles, overall sales in the relevant genres, QR scans, and whether customers buy a featured author again within 30 days. Ask staff to record only brief, voluntary customer comments about whether the comparison cards helped. Success would mean featured titles gain sales without reducing sales of established authors or creating frequent navigation questions. If the pilot works, expand it gradually to mystery, romance, and nonfiction, retaining genre-based placement and limiting each display to a small, easy-to-scan selection.
