Dust rose around Mara’s knees as she lifted the blue cup from a box of chipped plates. “Donation pile,” she said, setting it beside old curtains. Theo snatched it back so quickly the cup clinked against his watch. “It’s just a cup.” “Exactly.” She reached for it again, and he turned away, wiping its rim with his sleeve. Beneath the dust, a faint white sailboat appeared. Mara stopped. Theo carried it to the sink in the corner, ran a trickle of water, and rinsed out the dried leaves inside. He placed it carefully on the windowsill, where afternoon light filled it blue. Mara quietly moved the donation box downstairs, leaving the cup alone among the rafters.
