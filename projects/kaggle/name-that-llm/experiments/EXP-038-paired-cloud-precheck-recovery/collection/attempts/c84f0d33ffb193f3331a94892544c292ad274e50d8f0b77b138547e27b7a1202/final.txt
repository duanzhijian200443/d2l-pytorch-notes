Mara held up the unremarkable blue cup. “Keep it,” she said, placing it in Jonah’s box. He took it out and set it beside her suitcase. “You hate this thing.” “You always did.” She reached for it again, but Jonah closed his fingers around the handle. Dust drifted through the attic window as they faced each other among their mother’s labeled boxes. Mara glanced at the cup’s chipped rim, then began folding the old curtains instead of arguing. Jonah carried the cup downstairs. In the kitchen, he filled it from the tap and left it on the table, beside the chair where their mother used to sit. Mara appeared in the doorway, holding two spoons. Without speaking, she put one in the cup.
