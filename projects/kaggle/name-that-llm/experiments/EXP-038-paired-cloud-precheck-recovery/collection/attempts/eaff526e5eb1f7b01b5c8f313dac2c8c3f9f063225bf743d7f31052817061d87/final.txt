The first room begins with twelve chairs. When four chairs are moved to the second room, subtract four from twelve: 12 − 4 = 8, so eight chairs remain in the first room at that intermediate point. Next, three chairs are brought back from the second room into the first room. Add those three chairs to the eight already there: 8 + 3 = 11. Therefore, after the chairs are moved out and then some are returned, eleven chairs remain in the first room. Another way to see this is that only one chair is ultimately kept in the second room, since four left but three came back, so the first room has twelve minus one, which is eleven.
