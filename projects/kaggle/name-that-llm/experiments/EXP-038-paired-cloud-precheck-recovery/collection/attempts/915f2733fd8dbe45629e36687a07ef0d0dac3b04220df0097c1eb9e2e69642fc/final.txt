Place a washable doormat just inside the door and designate three slim shoe zones along the wall: one for each roommate, using existing trays, boxes, or reusable shopping bags. Keep only the pair each person wears most often at the entrance; store extra shoes in bedrooms or closets. Agree that shoes must stay within their owner’s zone, with wet footwear on the mat or a tray. Do a quick nightly reset so the doorway remains clear and the door opens fully.
