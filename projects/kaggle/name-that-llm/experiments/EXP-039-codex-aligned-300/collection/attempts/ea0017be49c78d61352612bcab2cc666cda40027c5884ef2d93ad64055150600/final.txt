A queue is a line where everyone waits for a turn. Imagine children waiting for ice cream: the first child in line gets served first, then the next child, and so on. Waiting can feel long when you really want something, especially if the counter is close. But without a line, everyone might rush forward, push past each other, and argue about who arrived first. The person at the counter would have trouble knowing whom to help. A queue gives everyone a simple rule to follow: join at the end, move forward when the person ahead is finished, and wait until it is your turn.
