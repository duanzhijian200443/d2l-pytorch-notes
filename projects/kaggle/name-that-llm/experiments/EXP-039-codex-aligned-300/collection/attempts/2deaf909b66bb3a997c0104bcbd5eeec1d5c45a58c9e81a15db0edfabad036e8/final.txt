A queue is a way for people to take turns when they all want the same thing. Everyone stands in a line and waits for the person in front to finish before moving forward. Waiting can feel slow, especially when you are eager to reach the counter, but the line keeps things clear and organized. Without a queue, everyone might try to get there first by pushing, squeezing, or rushing ahead. Then the strongest or most forceful person might reach the counter, while others could be left behind. A queue gives each person a place and a predictable turn. It is like saying, “You go first, then I go, and after me, the next person goes.”
