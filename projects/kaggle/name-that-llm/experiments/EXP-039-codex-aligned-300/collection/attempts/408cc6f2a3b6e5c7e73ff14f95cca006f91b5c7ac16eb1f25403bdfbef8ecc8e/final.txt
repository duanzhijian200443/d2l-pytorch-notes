A queue is a line of people waiting for their turns, like when you’re buying an ice cream. Everyone in the line is using the same simple plan: the person at the front goes next, and the others move forward as each turn finishes. When you really want your ice cream, waiting can feel slow. You might wonder why you can’t just walk straight to the counter. But imagine if everyone tried that at once! People would squeeze into the same spot, and whoever pushed hardest might get served first. The line keeps getting your ice cream from turning into a pushing contest. You still have to wait, but there’s a clear way to know when your turn is coming.
