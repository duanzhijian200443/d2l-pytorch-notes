A queue is a line of people waiting for the same thing, such as buying a snack or getting on a ride. Everyone agrees, without needing to talk about it, that the person who arrived first goes first, followed by the next person, and so on. Waiting in line can feel slow because each person has to wait for those ahead. But without a queue, everyone might rush toward the counter at once. People could bump into one another, argue about who was first, or try to squeeze through the crowd. That would make it harder for the worker to help anyone. A queue keeps the order clear, so people know when their turn is coming and the counter does not become a pushing contest.
