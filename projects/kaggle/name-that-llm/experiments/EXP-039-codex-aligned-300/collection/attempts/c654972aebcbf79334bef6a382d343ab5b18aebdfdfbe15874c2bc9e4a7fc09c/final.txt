For years, the old coat hung quietly at the back of the wardrobe, kept more from habit than use. It no longer fit, its sleeves recalling a smaller version of me. In one pocket I found a faded cinema ticket, and the film itself had vanished from memory. What remained was the friend beside me, our laughter before the lights went down. I placed the ticket in a drawer, then gave the coat away, letting both past and present make room for each other.
