You could say, “I’m always happy to help when I can, but I’ve noticed the tool came back later than we agreed the last couple of times. I often need it for my own projects, so in the future, could we agree on a specific return date before you borrow it? If it looks like you’ll need it longer, please check with me before that date so I can plan around it. If I know I’ll need the tool soon, I may have to say no or suggest another option. I value being good neighbors, and I hope this keeps borrowing simple and comfortable for both of us.” This is warm but clear, focuses on practical needs rather than blame, and sets a reasonable expectation for communication.
