Keep the boundary friendly, specific, and focused on getting your tools back when you need them: “I’m happy to help when I can, but the last couple of times, the tool came back later than we agreed. Going forward, I’ll need us to agree on a return day and time before I lend anything out. If that timing won’t work, no worries, but I’ll need to keep it here.” Choose deadlines that genuinely suit you, and feel free to decline when you can’t risk a delay. If another agreed deadline is missed, you can calmly say, “I’m going to take a break from lending tools, but I appreciate your understanding.” You can stay neighborly without continuing an arrangement that doesn’t work for you.
